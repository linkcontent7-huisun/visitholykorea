"Galgok-ri is a village of believers who settled here around 1896, fleeing persecution, and made their living firing earthenware. Father Benedict Kim Chi-ho, who grew up in this village, and his sister Sister Marianna Kim Jeong-suk were each martyred in 1950 and are remembered as witnesses of faith in the Korean War."

"Galgok-ri in Beobwon-eup, Paju, was called Chikul for the arrowroot that grew thickly there. Recorded in 1898 as the Chilul mission station under Yakhyeon parish, its twenty believers grew to one hundred forty-five within two years, becoming a community of faith in northern Gyeonggi."

"The old church collapsed in the Korean War and was rebuilt in 1955, modeled on Uijeongbu Church, with the help of Father Joseph, a U.S. Marine chaplain. In 2018 the Diocese of Uijeongbu designated the church a pilgrimage site in memory of Father Kim Chi-ho and Sister Kim Jeong-suk."
