"Jeongsan Shrine remembers the martyrdom of the potter Paul Yi Do-gi. Spreading the faith around Cheongyang and Jeongsan, he was arrested in 1797 and beaten to death at Jeongsan the following year. Unbending in his faith even in prison, he was declared blessed by Pope Francis in 2014."

"The shrine lies near the Jeongsan bus terminal in Seojeong-ri, Jeongsan-myeon, Cheongyang. The Jeongsan prison where Yi Do-gi was held and the marketplace where he was dragged out to confess his faith are the places that convey his last days. Parishioners of Cheongyang Church located the martyrdom site and created a memorial space for the 220th anniversary of his death."

"The large jar-shaped guide stone at the entrance shows that Yi Do-gi was a potter. At the chapel entrance lies a granite symbol of the cangue, the wooden collar fastened around prisoners' necks. The Paul Yi chapel, built in 2018 for the 220th anniversary, and the statue of Mary with outstretched hands convey the torture he suffered at Jeongsan and the devotion to Mary he kept to the end."
