"Sanmakgol is a village of believers in Seocheon where the faithful hid and lived together after the Gihae Persecution of 1839. Bishop Daveluy and Father Féron made it a base of their ministry. The family of St. Luke Hwang Seok-du also moved here from Yeonpung, and the life of faith before the Byeongin Persecution continued in this valley."

"Sanmakgol is in Geumdeok-ri, Pangyo-myeon, Seocheon, and Jageunjae is a pass on the slopes of Cheonbangsan. The pass links the Dongmoe station site in Suam-ri, Munsan-myeon, with the Jageunjae station site in Geumdeok-ri. The pass road, once holding the row graves of nameless believers, underwent road construction, and the path linking the old villages has been revived as a pilgrimage route."

"A Marian garden has been laid out at the Dongmoe station site, and the fourteen Stations of the Cross line the mountain path up to Jageunjae. The memorials at the two station sites and the stone at the Jageunjae row-grave site mark different traces. These facilities were blessed when a commemorative Mass was celebrated at the top of the pass in 2010."
