"Wondong parish, separated from Pungsuwon parish in 1896, was the first parish in the Wonju area. Its first pastor, Father Le Merre, bought land near the Wonju government office to found it. In the 1970s it became a center of the democracy movement, and when Bishop Ji Hak-sun was imprisoned in 1974, prayer meetings on the state of the nation continued in this church."

"The cathedral stands in Wondong, in downtown Wonju, Gangwon-do, close to the old government office. Wonju, seat of the provincial governor in the Joseon era, was also where believers were brought for interrogation during the persecutions. Built beside that office, the church grew alongside the city's history and became the cathedral of the Diocese of Wonju."

"The Gothic church completed in 1913 was consecrated by Bishop Mutel, but was heavily damaged in the Korean War and rebuilt in 1954. Its round domed bell tower is the face of the church, and in 2004 that distinctive form earned it designation as Registered Cultural Heritage No. 139."
