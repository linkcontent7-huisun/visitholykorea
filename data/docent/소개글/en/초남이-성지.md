"Chonami is the birthplace of Augustine Yu Hang-geom, the apostle of Honam, and of the virgin couple John Yu Jung-cheol and Lutgarda Yi Sun-i. The Yu family was martyred in the Sinyu Persecution of 1801, and their house was torn down under the sentence of demolition and turned into a pond."

"The shrine spans the village of Chonami and Baubaegi in Namgye-ri, Iseo-myeon, Wanju. Yu Hang-geom's house was the gathering place of the Jeolla community of faith, and in 2021 the remains of Yun Ji-chung, Kwon Sang-yeon and Yun Ji-heon were identified at Baubaegi."

"The Diocese of Jeonju bought the site of the demolished house in 1985 and consecrated the birthplace as a shrine. On the grounds the catechism hall where the Yu brothers studied and debated doctrine has been restored, and at Baubaegi a graveyard and memorial space for the three martyrs link the two places."
