"Bishop Demange, the first bishop of the diocese, pushed for a seminary to train local priests. A believer in Shanghai donated the building costs on condition that St. Justin be its patron, and the school opened in 1914; until it closed in 1945 it raised sixty-seven priests."

"The seminary was built on diocesan land in Namsan-dong, Daegu, donated by Augustine Seo Sang-don. Closed under Japanese pressure, it started again in 1982 as Seonmok Theological College, and today its lineage continues in the College of Theology of Daegu Catholic University."

"Father Poisnel, who took part in building Myeongdong Cathedral, designed the two-story red-brick building, and Chinese craftsmen built it. Its features are a symmetrical U-shaped plan with the chapel at the center and a continuous arched corridor on the ground floor; the surviving wings of the central section are used as a history hall."
