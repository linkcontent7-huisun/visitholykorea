"The Missionary Society of St. Columban, in charge of mission in Gangwon, built the bishop's residence of the Diocese of Chuncheon in 1958. The education center completed the following year began as the novitiate of the Sisters of Our Lady of Perpetual Help, training sisters to support the Gangwon mission."

"The two buildings stand side by side in the old center of Chuncheon, near Jungnim-dong Cathedral. The education center passed from a sisters' formation house to a women's college dormitory before becoming the Catholic Education Center in 1977, while the bishop's residence served as the bishop's home and the administrative hub of the Gangwon mission."

"The bishop's residence is unusual in facing west, following the building custom of Ireland, where sunlight is scarce. Preserving an exterior that recalls the homeland of the Columban missionaries and their 1950s structure, the two buildings were registered together as national cultural heritage in 2019."
