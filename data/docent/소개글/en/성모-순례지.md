"The Marian shrine of the Diocese of Incheon began with the petition of believers who wished for a place to pray to Mary. Bishop Choi Gi-san prepared the site and the design, and the building continued after his death. The groundbreaking was held in 2017, the centenary of the apparitions at Fatima, and the shrine was dedicated on 13 October of the following year."

"The Marian pilgrimage site is a shrine within the offices of the Diocese of Incheon, established in 1961, in Songnim-dong, Dong-gu, Incheon. Here the faithful pray for the reconciliation and unity of North and South together with the message of world peace given by Our Lady of Fatima. The prayer space on Bangmun-ro, where the diocesan offices stand, carries the intent to embrace both the division of the peninsula and the peace of families."

"The Marian shrine dedicated in 2018 is a domed building rising high at the center. In the middle is enshrined a statue of Mary made at Fatima in Portugal, the site of the apparitions of 1917. The round prayer space built in Incheon and the statue that came from Fatima across the sea meet here, conveying the origins of a building begun on the centenary of the apparitions."
