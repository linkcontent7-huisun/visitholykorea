"Myojae is the house where Augustine Nam Sang-gyo gave up public office and devoted himself to the life of faith. His son John Nam Jong-sam also received his father's teaching here. When the Byeongin Persecution broke out, Nam Jong-sam was caught on his way to Seoul and martyred outside Seosomun in 1866, and his father Nam Sang-gyo also lost his life at Gongju."

"The house stands in Haksan-ri, Bongyang-eup, Jecheon, in a village near the Haksan Bridge. It can be found on the road from Yongsomak Church toward Jecheon, and beside the house is the Haksan mission station. From the 1920s believers moved into this village where the Nam father and son had lived, forming a village of faith and establishing the station."

"The Nam family house was repaired with the help of descendants and benefactors, and in 2014 the building was dismantled and fully restored. The old station in front of the house was built in 1955, the new Haksan station in 1989. The statue of Mary behind the house and the Way of the Cross on the hill add a pilgrimage space to the home where the martyrs' family lived."
