"The Doejae community was founded by believers who gathered in the mountains of Hwasan-myeon, Wanju, to escape persecution. Father Villemot established the parish in 1891, and when the church was built in 1895 it became the first parish church south of the Han River and the center of a mountain village of believers."

"Doejae in Seungchi-ri, Wanju, is a village hidden inside a mountain pass. The believers brought timber from an abandoned temple in Nonsan to build the church, and villages of the faithful formed around it, making a settlement where church and daily life stood together."

"The church of 1895 was Korea's first hanok church, combining traditional timber structure with a Western liturgical space. It burned down in 1950 during the Korean War, but was restored from old photographs and records, and a blessing ceremony was held in 2009."
