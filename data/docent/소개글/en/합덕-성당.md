"Hapdeok parish began at the Yangchon mission station in Yesan in 1890 and moved to its present site in 1899. During the Korean War the pastor Father Perrin, the layman Yun Bok-su and the altar server Song Sang-won were captured and killed, and the martyrs' memorial in the churchyard remembers the three."

"The church stands on the Hapdeok hill overlooking the Naepo plain between the shrines of Solmoe, Sinri and Yeosaul. Rents from the surrounding farmland of the Vicariate of Seoul once helped run the Yongsan seminary, linking the old villages of Naepo and Hapdeok parish to the history of priestly formation."

"The red-brick Hapdeok Church, completed in 1929 under Father Perrin, has identical bell towers on either side of its facade. The pointed arch between the twin towers and the long side windows form a clear silhouette on the Naepo plain, and the church is preserved as Chungcheongnam-do Monument No. 145."
