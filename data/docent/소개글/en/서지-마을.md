"Seoji is the village where Blessed John Choe Hae-seong and his aunt Brigid Choe lived. In the Gihae Persecution of 1839, Choe Hae-seong sent his family to hide at Kkotdaengi in Baegun and returned to the village to fetch church books, where he was caught. The story of him and his aunt, martyred at the Wonju gamyeong, is also told in the letters of Father Choe Yang-eop."

"The village is in Songok-ri, Buron-myeon, Wonju. The name Seoji comes from the abundance of gromwell, a herb used to make purple dye. The family of Choe Hae-seong, from Darakgol in Hongju, Chungcheong, followed the grandfather to his place of exile, then settled here seeking neighbors with whom to share the faith, forming a small village of believers."

"The Seoji Village Martyrs' Memorial Church was built in white to express the self-emptying lives of the martyrs. The memorial hall remembers not only Choe Hae-seong and Brigid Choe but also the martyrs of the Diocese of Wonju known by name alone, and those who left no name. It is a space holding the diocese's history of martyrdom in the village where one family lived."
