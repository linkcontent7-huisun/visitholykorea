"Hwangmusil is an old village of believers in Naepo where the faithful lived from before the Sinhae Persecution. Fathers Maistre and Landre worked and died here, and Father Huin also cared for the believers. It is also the home ground of Francis Yi Bo-hyeon, martyred at Haemi, and his beatification set the development of the shrine in earnest."

"Hwangmusil in Seogu-ri, Hapdeok-eup, Dangjin, lies on low hills bordering Yesan; Father Maistre died here in 1857 and Father Landre in 1863. When little seawater reached the Sapgyo stream, people of Hongju and Deoksan walked past here to Namwonpo to take a boat. Its position close to the early communities of faith such as Sinri and Yeosaul supported the missionaries' work."

"Behind the outdoor altar at Hwangmusil stands a martyrs' memorial of natural stone, with statues of Jesus and Mary beside it. It is a space that Sinhapdeok parish developed after buying the land of the old village, and a Mass of blessing was celebrated in 2014. The memory of the village where Blessed Yi Bo-hyeon lived has been revived around the stone monument and the altar."
