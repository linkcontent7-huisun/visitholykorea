"Pungsuwon is a village of believers formed after the Sinyu Persecution, when Peter Sin Tae-bo and other faithful took refuge here. Even in years when no priest could stay with them, the believers relied on one another and kept the faith. In 1888 Father Le Merre arrived and the parish was founded. A small mountain community grew into the heart of the faith in Gangwon."

"The church is in the mountain village of Yuhyeon-ri, Seowon-myeon, Hoengseong, Gangwon-do. People fleeing persecution from Seoul, Gyeonggi and elsewhere gathered to live in this valley. At first several thatched houses were joined to serve as a church. Looking at the mountains and village around today's brick church, you recall a community that began as a refuge."

"The church built under Father Jeong Gyu-ha uses red and grey brick. Inside, pillars stand in rows and the rounded ceiling is made of wood rather than stone — traditional timber craft applied to a Western-style church. The old rectory beside it has become a history hall, showing the community's years alongside the church."
