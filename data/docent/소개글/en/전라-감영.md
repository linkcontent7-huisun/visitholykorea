"Jeolla Gamyeong was the office where the provincial governor administered Jeolla Province, together with the Jeonju prefectural office and prison. In the Sinhae Persecution of 1791, Paul Yun Ji-chung and James Kwon Sang-yeon were interrogated here and became the first martyrs of the Korean Church."

"In 1801 the family of Augustine Yu Hang-geom and Francis Yun Ji-heon were tried by the gamyeong, and in 1827 Paul Yi Gyeong-eon. Peter Sin Tae-bo in 1839 and Peter Son Seon-ji in 1866, among others, also passed through this office."

"The gamyeong buildings on the site of the former Jeollabuk-do provincial office were partly restored around Seonhwadang in 2020. In the restored compound one can trace the interrogations repeated across the persecutions and the route of martyrdom that led to the Jeonju prison and the execution ground outside Pungnammun."
