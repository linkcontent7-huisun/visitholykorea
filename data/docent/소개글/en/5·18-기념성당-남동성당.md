"Namdong Church opened in 1949 as Gwangju's second parish, separated from Bukdong Church. In May 1980, Father Kim Seong-yong and civic leaders formed a settlement committee here, and Father Kim carried news of Gwangju's tragedy to Cardinal Kim Sou-hwan."

"Near the former South Jeolla Provincial Office in Namdong, Gwangju, the church continued prayers for the release of detainees and memorial Masses after the uprising. From 1981, priests of the Archdiocese of Gwangju and citizens gathered every year until the crowds filled the church, its grounds and the nearby roads."

"In 2005 Namdong Church became Gwangju's twenty-fifth May 18 historic site and the diocese's May 18 Memorial Church. The Marian garden in the churchyard is shaped after Mudeungsan, and the statue of Mary embracing Gwangju, together with bullet marks on the outer wall, carries the memory of faith and of the citizens' uprising."
