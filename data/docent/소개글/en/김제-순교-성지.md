"Gimje Martyrs' Shrine lies within the grounds of Yochon Church and honors Stanislaus Han Jeong-heum, a martyr born in Gimje. A poor nobleman, he was baptized by Peter Yi Seung-hun in 1784 and then spread the gospel in his hometown."

"Han Jeong-heum was arrested in the Sinyu Persecution of 1801, interrogated at the Jeolla provincial office and sent to the capital. Refusing to renounce his faith, he was beheaded on 26 August of that year at the age of forty-five, bearing witness to his faith."

"The memorial space at Yochon Church presents Han Jeong-heum's life together with the roots of the early Church in the Gimje area. It is a shrine that retraces, in his birthplace of Gimje, his final journey from the Jeonju provincial office to the execution ground in Seoul."
