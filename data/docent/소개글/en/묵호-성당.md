"Mukho became a parish in 1948, and the following year Father Patrick Reilly from Ireland was appointed. When the Korean War broke out the believers urged him to flee, but he stayed in Mukho. Captured by North Korean troops, he lost his life on the road while being taken to Gangneung."

"The church near Mukho Station in Donghae neighbors the fishing village of Mukho harbor. On the lighthouse hill behind the harbor runs Nongoldam-gil, whose murals record the life of residents who carried fish up the slope. The Mukho community of faith settled in this neighborhood of people moving between harbor and railway."

"The church completed in 1957 has blue-gray walls trimmed with white lines. It is one of the churches the Diocese of Chuncheon built to commemorate priests martyred in the war. In the yard stands the memorial to Father Patrick Reilly raised in 1974, conveying the purpose for which the building was erected."
