"Peter Son Seon-ji and Bartholomew Jeong Mun-ho were born in Jiseok-ri, Buyeo, and martyred at Supjeongi in Jeonju in 1866. Son Seon-ji offered his house as a mission station and led the believers, encouraging his companions even in prison. The two were declared saints together in 1984."

"Jiseok-ri was a village of Imcheon in Chungcheong called Goeindol, and now belongs to Chunghwa-myeon, Buyeo. The site honoring the two saints' hometown lies beside the Palchungsa shrine. Since they were martyred at Supjeongi in Jeonju and their remains rest at Cheonho Shrine, Jiseok-ri is a shrine that remembers the land of their birth and upbringing."

"After descendants of St. Son Seon-ji donated a field, Hongsan Church set about developing the shrine. On village land secured because the exact birthplace could not be found, a birth memorial and an outdoor altar were raised in 1988. In 2014 a new memorial was erected, and a marker stone and parking area were added."
