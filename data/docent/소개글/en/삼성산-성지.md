"Bishop Imbert and Fathers Maubant and Chastan were martyred at Saenamteo in the Gihae Persecution of 1839. In 1843 Paul Bak secretly moved the remains of the three clergy, buried at Nogosan, to his family's ancestral hill on the slopes of Gwanaksan."

"Paul Bak's son Bak Sun-jip testified to the location of the graves, and in 1901 the remains were transferred by way of the Yongsan seminary to the crypt of Myeongdong Cathedral. Because the three martyred clergy lay buried here for fifty-eight years, the mountain came to be called Samseongsan, the mountain of three saints."

"The shrine keeps memorials raised in 1970 and 1981 and the empty graves of the three clergy. Imbert, Maubant and Chastan were canonized by Pope John Paul II in 1984, the bicentenary of the Catholic Church in Korea."
