"Yanggeun is the home where the brothers Kwon Cheol-sin and Kwon Il-sin were born and led the early community of faith. After Yi Seung-hun returned from baptism in Beijing in 1784, the faith spread from here to Chungcheong and Jeolla, and Yun Yu-il, Yun Jeom-hye, Kwon Sang-mun and others were beatified in 2014."

"The shrine lies near the sandbar at Omitdari in Yangpyeong, where the Yanggeun stream meets the Namhan River. The name Yanggeun, meaning willow root, comes from the riverside trees that protected the village in floods, and the sandbar is said to be where believers such as Yun Yu-o and Yun Jeom-hye were beheaded."

"At Yanggeun Shrine it is the waters of the Namhan River and the martyrdom site, rather than a grand church, that speak first. The shrine space on the Mulangae Park path joins the hometown of Kwon Cheol-sin and Kwon Il-sin to the execution site at Omitdari in one route, honoring the names of the early Church's founders."
