"The Sinam-ri community of faith grew from a village of believers who gathered to escape persecution in the late Joseon era. The official history of the church begins with the founding of a mission station under Gaeseong parish in 1907. It became a parish in 1925, reverted to a station in 1930, and after many years returned to full parish status in 2013."

"The church stands on a village hill in Sinam-ri, Nam-myeon, Yangju, where the wind blows from Gamaksan. This village is the hometown where Father Laurence Yi Chun-geun of the Benedictines was born and raised. Pilgrims who remember the life of the priest martyred at Pyongyang in 1950 come to seek the traces of the Sinam-ri village."

"The parish history records the building of an education hall and rectory in 2008 and a Mass of thanksgiving for the completion of the church in 2015. These are the buildings raised while Sinam-ri, long a station, settled again as a parish. The statue of Mary in the churchyard is where pilgrims pray who have come to this small community on the slopes of Gamaksan."
