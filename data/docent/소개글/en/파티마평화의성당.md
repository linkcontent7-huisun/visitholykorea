"The Korean headquarters of the World Apostolate of Fatima pursued from 1982 the building of a church to pray for the peaceful reunification of the Korean Peninsula. After waiting some thirty years because of military operation zones and changes of site, the church was consecrated in 2015 with Bishop Yi Gi-heon presiding."

"The church stands at Majeong-ri in Munsan, Paju, near Imjingak, and is counted as the prayer place closest to the armistice line. Because of height limits protecting military installations, the sanctuary was placed underground so that it would hardly show above the surface."

"The building has two floors below ground and two above, and the underground sanctuary holds devotion to Our Lady of Fatima and prayer for peace between North and South. The Sisters of the Immaculate Heart of Mary manage the space, and the church centers on the statue of Our Lady of Fatima and the rosary."
