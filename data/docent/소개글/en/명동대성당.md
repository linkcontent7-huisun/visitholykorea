"Myeongdong Cathedral traces its roots to the Myeongnyebang community, where Joseon believers gathered to share their faith. The Church that endured persecution consecrated the cathedral in 1898 and later placed the remains of martyrs in the crypt. As the mother church of the Archdiocese of Seoul, it also holds the memory of those who kept the faith."

"From the busy streets of Myeongdong, a climb up the hill brings you to the cathedral yard. Its old name, Jonghyeon Cathedral, came from the name of this hill, and after liberation it became Myeongdong Cathedral. The hill that remains between shops and tall buildings is a place that joins the city's daily life to a space of prayer."

"After Father Coste, who designed the church, passed away, Father Poisnel completed the work. It is a Gothic brick cathedral marked by pointed arches and a bell tower. The tall windows of the sanctuary carry the life of the Virgin Mary, telling the story of faith through the light that passes through them."
