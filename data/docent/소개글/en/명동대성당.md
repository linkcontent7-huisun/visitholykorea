"Completed in 1898, the first Gothic brick church in Korea. Historic Site No. 258, and a landmark of the 1987 democracy movement."

"The site stands at 74 Myeongdong-gil, Jung-gu, Seoul. The crypt below holds the remains of the first bishops of the Korean Church."

"Inside, light falling through the stained glass carries the weight of the hundred and twenty years held in each grey brick."
