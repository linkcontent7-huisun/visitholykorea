"Geumgwang-ri is a village where believers who moved to Gangneung to escape the Byeongin Persecution kept their faith. They lived by firing pottery and farming, and founded a mission station in 1887. Priests came by boat from Wonsan and Anbyeon to administer the sacraments."

"The station is on Geumpyeong-ro in Gujeong-myeon, Gangneung, administratively part of Eodan-ri, so it was also called the Eodan-ri station. In this community, cared for by Noam-dong Church, the stories of ancestors who fled persecution from Gyeongju and Yeoju survive in the testimony of the station leader's family."

"The station building is said to have been moved from its original site toward the road in the 1950s. Above the front door is carved the founding year, 1887. Inside remain a wooden floor, a pine-board altar and long benches with kneelers, showing the worship carried on in a small station."
