"Father Cho Seon-hui, an Australian, came to Korea in 1940 and was expelled by the Japanese; during the Korean War he was taken prisoner and endured the death march. After fifty-eight years of ministry in the Diocese of Chuncheon, he founded this retreat house for atonement and peace in 1990."

"The retreat house lies in Nam-myeon, Inje, in a mountain valley once crossed by the 38th parallel. Father Cho created a place to pray for peace in the very land where he had lived through war and division, and the deep mountains of Inje, stretching to the Naerin stream and Seoraksan, form the backdrop for silent retreats."

"The fourteen Stations of the Cross on the grounds were cast in bronze by the sculptor Cho Suk-ui, preserving the texture of clay. Blessed by Bishop Jang Ik in 2013, each station was donated by the bishop and benefactors, and the outdoor prayer spaces and retreat facilities carry on Father Cho's intent."
