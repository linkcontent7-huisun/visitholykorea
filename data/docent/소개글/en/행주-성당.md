"The Haengju community of faith began in 1899 as a mission station under Yakhyeon parish and became a parish in 1909. Father Kim Won-yeong was its first pastor; after the parish center moved to Sinchon in 1957 it became a station, and with the establishment of the Diocese of Uijeongbu in 2004 it became a parish again."

"The church stands in Haengju-oe-ri, Goyang, along the waterway of the Han River. When the Haengju station was founded at this hub of transport and trade linking Tongjin, Bupyeong and Ganghwa, the believers grew from fifty-two in the first year to a hundred and two in the next, and it became the center of faith for a riverside trading village."

"The Korean-style wooden church dedicated in 1910 was moved to its present site in 1928. It holds a Western church plan within a tiled roof and timber structure, and for the value of its well-preserved century-old form it became National Registered Cultural Heritage No. 455 in 2010."
