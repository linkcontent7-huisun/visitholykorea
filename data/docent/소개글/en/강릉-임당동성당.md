"Gangneung parish, the first in the Gangneung area, began in 1921 at Geumgwang-ri and moved several times. After the Korean War, the present church was completed in Imdang-dong in 1955, restoring the center of the local community."

"The exterior is composed of a tall bell tower, pointed-arch windows and buttresses supporting the walls. Fine molding survives inside, showing the typical form of church architecture in the Gangwon region in the 1950s."

"When Okcheon-dong Church separated in 1974, the heritage name changed from Gangneung Church to Imdang-dong Church. The building is registered as National Registered Cultural Heritage No. 457, recognized for the value of preserving postwar Catholic architecture."
