"Father Kim Taegon's family settled at Golbaemasil in Yongin in 1827 to escape persecution. The boy Kim Taegon grew up in this mountain valley, and in 1836 he was chosen as a seminarian by Father Maubant, beginning the road to study in Macau and to become Korea's first priest."

"Golbaemasil is a village of believers hidden in a valley beneath Galmibong, east of Eunyi Shrine. The village where Kim Taegon's grandfather Kim Taek-hyeon and father Kim Je-jun lived was linked by mountain paths to the Eunyi and Mirinae villages, forming a network of faith in southern Gyeonggi during the persecutions."

"The shrine holds the site of the boy Kim Taegon's home, a stone statue, and a sculpture of his mother Ursula Go pounding grain in a mortar. The Kim Taegon memorial pavilion built in 2024 revives the form of the Namgok-ri Church memorial chapel that stood at Beolteo in 1947."
