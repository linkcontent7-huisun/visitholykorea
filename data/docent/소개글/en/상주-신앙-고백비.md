"The Confession of Faith Stone in Cheongni-myeon, Sangju, is the only monument in the Korean Church on which a believer, Dominic Kim Sam-rok, carved his own prayers into granite. He kept his faith even after his brothers abandoned it in the Byeongin Persecution of 1866."

"From 1894 to 1900, after the persecutions had ended, Kim Sam-rok carved into the rock prayers for God, the pope, the bishop, the priests and his fellow believers. The tree that hid the stone was cut down in 1945 by his grandson Kim Sun-gyeong, bringing it to light again."

"A survey by Father O Gi-seon in 1984 confirmed it as a church historic site, and in 2009 it was designated Cultural Heritage Material No. 562 of Gyeongsangbuk-do. Around it stand a large cross, an altar and a sculpture for the Great Jubilee of 2000."
