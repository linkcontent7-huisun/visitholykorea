"Tongjeyeong in Tongyeong was the headquarters of the naval forces of the three southern provinces and an office where Catholics were interrogated. Felix Peter Kim Gi-ryang, a merchant from Jeju, was arrested on Ge Island in 1866 and imprisoned here."

"Kim Gi-ryang did not renounce his faith under harsh interrogation, and in 1867 he was martyred by hanging in the Tongjeyeong prison. Called the apostle of Jeju for bringing the faith to the island, he was beatified by Pope Francis in 2014."

"Besides Kim Gi-ryang, many believers bore witness to their faith at Tongjeyeong, and Blessed Joseph Yun Bong-mun was also interrogated here in 1888. The path from Sebyeonggwan hall to the old prison site links the Joseon naval office with the history of martyrdom."
