"The interpreter Thomas Kim Beom-u offered his house at Myeongnyebang as a meeting place of faith for Yi Byeok, Jeong Yak-yong and others. Arrested in the Eulsa incident of 1785, he was exiled to Danjang in Miryang and died of the after-effects of torture, earning the name of the first confessor of the Korean Church."

"The shrine lies on the slopes of Maneosan in Yongjeon-ri, Samnangjin-eup, Miryang. When descendants' testimony and household registers showed that his place of exile was Danjang in Miryang rather than Danyang, the martyrs' commission of the Diocese of Busan located Kim Beom-u's grave on this mountain in 1989."

"Below the grave are a Marian grotto church, an outdoor altar and a Way of the Cross. The Diocese of Busan laid out the outdoor space in 2005, the grotto chapel in 2011, and an education hall and retreat house in 2017, turning the rediscovered grave into a pilgrimage shrine."
