"Sanjeong-dong in Mokpo is the base of mission in southwestern Korea, where the first parish in South Jeolla was founded in 1897. Father Deshayes, the first pastor, built up the community, and a red-brick church was erected in 1913."

"The church honors the martyrdom of Fathers Leonard, Augustine and Patrick, who were arrested and killed during the Korean War. An exhibition and a prayer space remembering the lives of the three missionaries are set within the shrine."

"In 2021 Sanjeong-dong Church became the first minor basilica in Korea approved by the Holy See. With its tall bell tower and red-brick exterior, the memorial church tells in one place the beginnings of the Mokpo parish and the history of wartime martyrdom."
