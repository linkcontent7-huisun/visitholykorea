"Jungnim-dong Church started as Chuncheon parish in 1920 and became the central church of the Chuncheon prefecture established in 1939. Since the diocese was raised in 1962, it has served as the cathedral holding the bishop's chair."

"Father Thomas Kong, appointed during the Korean War, largely completed the stone church in 1953 with support from the U.S. Army and the Holy See. Consecrated in 1956, the building is a postwar church of granite walls and a central bell tower."

"For its value as 1950s stone church architecture, the church became National Registered Cultural Heritage No. 54 in 2003. The clergy graveyard on the grounds and the cathedral together preserve the founding of the Diocese of Chuncheon and its rebuilding after the war."
