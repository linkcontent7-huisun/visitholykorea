"The Guryong village of believers was formed by those who fled the Eulhae Persecution of 1815. Peter Kim Yeong-je, arrested and released during the Byeongin Persecution, also nurtured a community of faith at Jaingol, and Father Robert's report of 1887 recorded sixty believers."

"The mission station lies near the summit of Guryongsan, where Unmun-myeon in Cheongdo, Bugan-myeon in Yeongcheon and Yongseong-myeon in Gyeongsan meet. Deep in the mountains, this place called the first village under heaven was a refuge from persecution and became a mountain community where believers from three counties gathered."

"In 1921 Bishop Demange named St. Mary Major in Rome its patron and consecrated it as a quasi-parish. The station was rebuilt in 1997, and in 2018 it was blessed as a heritage site of faith with the station, a relic hall and a retreat house, preserving the old mountain community."
