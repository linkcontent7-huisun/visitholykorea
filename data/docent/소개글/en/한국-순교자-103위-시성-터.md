"On 6 May 1984, Pope John Paul II declared 103 Korean martyrs saints, including Father Andrew Kim Taegon and Paul Jeong Ha-sang. About a million people gathered in Yeouido Plaza to witness the canonization together with the faith rally for the bicentenary of the Korean Catholic Church."

"The canonization site corresponds to the lawn of today's Yeouido Park in Yeongdeungpo-gu, Seoul. On Yeouido, from which Jeoldusan and Saenamteo lie across the Han River, the canonization was held not in Rome but in the martyrs' homeland, looking toward the ground where their blood was shed during the persecutions."

"The broad Yeouido Plaza has become a park, and the altar and event facilities of that day no longer remain. A memorial stone raised by the Korean martyrs' commission in 2009, on the 25th anniversary of the canonization, inscribes the site of 1984 and the names of the 103 saints in today's park."
