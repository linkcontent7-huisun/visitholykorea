"Majae is the village where the brothers Jeong Yak-jong and Jeong Yak-yong were born. Jeong Yak-jong, martyred for keeping his faith, was beatified in 2014, and his son Jeong Ha-sang and daughter Jeong Jeong-hye, born here, were canonized in 1984. The shrine took the name Holy Family Shrine to remember the faith and martyrdom of one household together."

"The shrine in Neungnae-ri, Joan-myeon, Namyangju, lies close to the Dasan historic site. Nearby are the restored birthplace of Jeong Yak-yong, the Yeoyudang, and the Silhak Museum, and Yanggeun and Cheonjinam, centers of the early Church, are not far. In the hometown of the Jeong brothers who shared their learning, one meets the background from which the Korean Church sprouted."

"The Thomas Church and the Myeongnyebang hall were built in traditional hanok style in 2007. After the Diocese of Uijeongbu sent a dedicated priest to develop the shrine, a blessing was held in 2008, and the space was renewed on the occasion of the beatification and canonization of Jeong Yak-jong's family. In 2017 the Majae Holy Family Shrine was proclaimed and blessed."
