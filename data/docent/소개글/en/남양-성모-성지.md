"Namyang-ri in Hwaseong is where believers were arrested and martyred in the Byeongin Persecution of 1866, and in 1991 it was consecrated as the first Marian shrine of the Korean Church. It honors the faith of the nameless martyrs of Namyang together with devotion to Mary."

"Along the shrine's Rosary Path lie stone rosary beads seventy centimeters across, set in the order of the twenty decades. The Garden of the Merciful Jesus, created in 2006, joins this prayer path to form the pilgrimage route."

"The Namyang Basilica of Our Lady, consecrated in 2023, was designed by the Swiss architect Mario Botta. Its two round towers and the light-filled sanctuary carry the shrine's prayer for the reunification of North and South."
