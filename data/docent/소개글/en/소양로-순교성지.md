"During the Byeongin Persecution of 1866, Catholics arrested in the Gangwon region were executed at Soyang-ro in Chuncheon. Soyang-ro Martyrs' Shrine remembers together those whose names were not recorded and the missionaries of the Diocese of Chuncheon who lost their lives in wartime."

"The shrine sits on the hill of Mosumul-gil, where downtown Chuncheon meets the Soyang River. With the riverside road near Chuncheon Station bordering the martyrdom site, it reveals the history of the Byeongin Persecution hidden within the landscape of the lakeside city."

"The Soyang-ro pilgrimage church on Mosumul-gil and the martyrs' memorial form the center of the shrine. The small church hosts the pilgrimage Masses of the Diocese of Chuncheon, and the memorial on the hill facing the river marks the place where the believers of Gangwon were executed in 1866."
