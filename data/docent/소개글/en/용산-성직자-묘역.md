"The remains of Bishop Imbert and Fathers Maubant and Chastan, martyred at Saenamteo in 1839, passed through Nogosan and were buried at Samseongsan in 1843. From 1885 to 1887 Father Poisnel investigated the location of the graves."

"In 1901 the remains of the three clergy were moved from Samseongsan to the clergy cemetery of the Sacred Heart Seminary in Yongsan. The seminary's professors and students tended the graves and honored the martyrdom of the missionaries who had worked in Joseon."

"On 2 November of that year the remains were transferred again to the crypt of Myeongdong Cathedral. Though they rested at Yongsan only briefly, the cemetery is the midpoint linking the secret burial ground of Samseongsan to the official tomb in the archdiocese's central church."
