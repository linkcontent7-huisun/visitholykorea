"Geodu-ri parish was founded on 27 September 2006, separated from Hyoja-dong parish and the Aemakgol chapel. The first community began with 376 households; with St. Vincent de Paul as its patron, it serves the southern Chuncheon area together with the Sisters of Notre Dame."

"The church is on Oesol-gil in Dongnae-myeon, Chuncheon, Gangwon-do. At its founding the parish covered the area east of the Seoksa crossroads and north of the Gongji stream, and parts of Wonchang-ri, Bongpyeong-ri, Joyang-ri and Gunja-ri; today it also oversees the Gomsil mission station, the cradle of the Diocese of Chuncheon."

"The community first rented a two-story building in Seoksa-dong, received liturgical items from other parishes, and celebrated its first Mass in November 2006. It later built a new church, dedicated on 3 October 2013 by Bishop Kim Un-hoe after the blessing of the Marian statue and the opening of the church doors."
