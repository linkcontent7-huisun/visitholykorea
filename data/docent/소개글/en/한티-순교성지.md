"At Hanti, believers fleeing persecution built huts and formed a village, firing pottery and burning charcoal. By the 1850s it had grown into a large community, but during the Byeongin Persecution it was raided by constables; the martyrs died here and were buried close to where they had lived."

"Hanti in Dongmyeong-myeon, Chilgok, lies in deep mountains more than 600 meters above sea level between Gasan and Palgongsan. It was a refuge 24 kilometers north of Daegu, and the family of Joachim Kim Hyeon-sang, who moved from Sinnamugol, also settled in this valley."

"The Archdiocese of Daegu surveyed the graves and secured land from the 1980s. A retreat house followed in 1991 and the restoration of the old mission station in 1998, and a pilgrims' church was blessed in 2004; the graves of unknown martyrs and three pilgrimage paths link the site of the village."
