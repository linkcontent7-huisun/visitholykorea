"Baengnyeong Island is where Father Kim Taegon in 1846 entrusted letters and maps to Chinese fishing boats while seeking a route for missionaries to enter Korea. Later, families of believers who moved from Jangyeon in Hwanghae Province gathered here and founded a mission station in 1947. The community, which prayed in a hall converted from Yi Ho-yeon's warehouse, grew into a parish in 1959."

"The pilgrimage site of martyr devotion is at Baengnyeongdo Church in Jinchon-ri, Baengnyeong-myeon, Ongjin. Because of its situation as an island in the West Sea far from the mainland, the parish placed stations in several villages. The first pastor, Father Moffett, founded stations not only on Baengnyeong but on Daecheong and Socheong islands, caring for believers separated by the sea."

"When Baengnyeong parish began, Father Moffett acquired a building owned by the township office for the church and rectory. In 1960 he founded the Blessed Andrew Kim Hospital beside the church. Later running a home for the elderly and an orphanage as well, this site served both worship and the medical care and welfare of the islanders."
