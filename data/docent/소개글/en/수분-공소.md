"Believers lived in Subun-ri from before the Byeongin Persecution, and after it the faithful who fled from across the country formed a community. In the 1850s Father Thomas Choe Yang-eop and Bishop Daveluy ministered in this area, and the mission station was founded in 1893."

"The station is in Subun-ri, Jangsu-eup, Jangsu, Jeollabuk-do, a mountain village on the Baekdu-daegan range. When it was built in 1921, the believers carried roof tiles from Hamyang and cut timber in the nearby forest, raising the church of their remote village with their own hands."

"Subun station is a building that wraps a Western-style worship plan in the timber frame and tiled roof of a hanok. Raised to a parish in 1926, it later became a station of Jangsu parish, and after designation as National Registered Cultural Heritage No. 189 in 2005 it has been under restoration since 2011."
