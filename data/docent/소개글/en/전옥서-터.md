"The Jeonokseo was the prison that held offenders interrogated by the Hyeongjo until their sentences were carried out. Saints Peter Yi Ho-yeong and Barbara Kim were martyred here after harsh imprisonment, and Yu Hang-geom and Yun Ji-heon, transported from Jeonju in 1801, were also confined here."

"The old prison site is in Seorin-dong, Jongno-gu, east of the Gwanghwamun intersection, in the city center between Jonggak Station and the Cheonggye stream. With the Hyeongjo, the Uigeumbu and the Left and Right Police Bureaus nearby, the routes by which believers of the persecution years passed from arrest, interrogation and imprisonment to the Seosomun execution ground overlap here."

"The prison building has vanished, but a marker of the Seoul Catholic pilgrimage route identifies the location of the Jeonokseo. The police-bureau prison pilgrimage route starting from Jongno Church passes the Left Police Bureau and the Uigeumbu, then this site, and continues through central Seoul to the Right Police Bureau and Hyeongjo sites and on to Myeongdong Cathedral."
