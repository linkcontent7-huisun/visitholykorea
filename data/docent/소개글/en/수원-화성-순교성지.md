"During the Byeongin Persecution, believers were interrogated and martyred at the prison and the military headquarters of Suwon Hwaseong, and at the marketplaces outside Paldalmun and Janganmun gates. Hwaseong Haenggung palace and the Hwacheonggwan office were also confirmed as places of witness, so that several sites within the fortress make up one history of martyrdom."

"The martyrdom sites are scattered inside Suwon Hwaseong, begun by King Jeongjo in 1794 and completed in 1796. Buksu-dong Church stands near the site of the old police bureau and serves as the center of an urban pilgrimage linking the palace, the markets and the prison site."

"Father Desideratus Polly of the Paris Foreign Missions built a Gothic church at Buksu-dong in 1932 to commemorate the Suwon martyrs. The bell tower at the church entrance marks the memory of the old police bureau, and the red-brick church, standing alongside the Joseon fortress, shows the beginnings of the modern Suwon parish."
