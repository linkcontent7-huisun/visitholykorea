"Hong Yu-han, known by his pen name Nongeun, read The True Meaning of the Lord of Heaven and the Seven Victories around 1750 while studying practical learning under Yi Ik. Without baptism or a priest, he kept the seventh, fourteenth, twenty-first and twenty-eighth of each month as a Sunday, practicing his faith through prayer and fasting."

"In 1775 Hong Yu-han moved to Gugu-ri below Sobaeksan in Yeongju and lived his last ten years like a hermit. The site of the old house where a scholar from a distinguished Seoul family settled after abandoning public office is the place where his journey south and his life of devotion actually unfolded."

"At the old house site in Gugu-ri, Dansan-myeon, the place of his daily life and a memorial marker remain. The faith that began here led to the martyrdom of thirteen descendants, and the brothers Peter Hong Byeong-ju and Paul Hong Yeong-ju became two of the 103 saints."
