"Jangdaebeol was one of the execution grounds where serious offenders were put to death in Daegu in the Joseon era, and a martyrdom site where Catholics lost their lives during the persecutions. Handed down as one of the three execution grounds in the Daegu area, it shows the history of persecution together with Gwandeokjeong and the Gyeongsang Gamyeong."

"The site is in Bongdeok-dong, Nam-gu, Daegu, within the Bongdeok neighborhood north of Apsan. Today's Bongdeok parish also stands on Daedeok-ro in the same Bongdeok-dong, so the local church keeps close the memory of the vanished execution ground."

"Because Jangdaebeol remembers the site of an old execution ground, the place itself, rather than an old church building, is the heart of it. Visitors should consider together its character as a martyrdom site surviving in the city center of Bongdeok-dong and the fact that it was one of the three execution grounds of Daegu."
