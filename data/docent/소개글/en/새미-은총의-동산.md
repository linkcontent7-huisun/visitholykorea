"Saemi Hill of Grace in Geumak-ri, Jeju, is a pilgrimage site laid out around the Saemiso volcanic cone and its pond. Work began in 1992, and in 2009 Bishop Kang U-il blessed it under its present name."

"Around the pond runs a rosary path of fifteen decades, followed by a shrine park presenting the life of Jesus in twelve scenes. On the life-size fourteen Stations of the Cross, visitors can also experience carrying the cross themselves."

"The Holy Trinity Church was built on the form of a Celtic cross and embraces a Marian grotto and a labyrinth prayer path. The name Saemi carries the hope that, like a spring, grace and the prayers of pilgrims will keep flowing."
