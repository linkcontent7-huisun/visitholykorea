"Hongcheon Church began as the Songjeong mission station cared for by Pungsuwon parish. It became a parish in 1923 with the appointment of Father Hwang Jeong-su. After the Korean War, Father Choe Dong-o began rebuilding the church, and Father Crosby, returning from internment, carried on the work."

"Songjeong-ri, where the parish first stood, was far from the town of Hongcheon. Father Kim Gyeong-min sought a new place where the faithful could gather, secured land in Huimang-ri, and the parish moved in 1936. The present church site on Majigi-ro in Hongcheon-eup holds the history of a parish that came out into the town."

"With the help of a U.S. Army engineer unit and the faithful, the stone church and rectory were completed in 1955. The outer walls are distinctive for stones grooved and fitted into one another. Beneath the wooden floor a wide empty space was left and coils of straw rope laid down to keep out moisture."
