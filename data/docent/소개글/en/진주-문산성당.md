"The history of Munsan Church begins with a community of believers who settled here to escape persecution. Faith that took root in the old village of Sochon grew through a mission station into a parish founded in 1905. As the congregation grew, Father Kim Yeong-je sold his property and his motorcycle to help build a church, and the parishioners joined their strength to raise it."

"The church sits on a low hill in Munsan-eup, Jinju. The site once held the office of the chalbang, the official who managed the post station; after the Church bought it, it became a place of prayer. In the wide yard a hanok church and a Western-style church still stand together, with a zelkova tree and lawn linking the view between them."

"Beside the tiled roof of the hanok church stands the Western-style church consecrated in 1937, completed by parishioners who carried the building materials themselves. Inside, pillars divide the space into a center and two sides, and behind the altar a painting of Christ with the twelve apostles shows the faith and the hands of different eras."
