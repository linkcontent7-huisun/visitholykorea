"On the night of 12 October 1845, the party of Father Kim Taegon, returning from Shanghai as a newly ordained priest after drifting to Jeju, landed here at Hwangsanpo. There were fourteen in all, including Bishop Ferréol and Father Daveluy. That night Father Kim celebrated his first Mass at the house of the believer Gu Sun-o, where he stayed for about a month and began his first ministry in his homeland."

"Ganggyeong was an old river port where the Geum River flowed on toward the sea. Because boats came and went here, it suited priests who had slipped ashore both for hiding and for setting out again. Bishop Ferréol also hid here for more than two months, carrying out his first duties as Vicar Apostolic of Joseon."

"The present church was completed in 1961 and, for its interior of continuous pointed arches, became Registered Cultural Heritage No. 650 in 2015. Beside it, a memorial hall modeled on the Jinjiaxiang church in Shanghai, where Father Kim was ordained, was built in 2019, bringing together the place where he became a priest and the place where his ministry began."
