"In the 1850s Luke Hwang Seok-du moved his family to Sapti so that his adopted son John Hwang Cheon-il and nephew Andrew Hwang Gi-won could care for the Sapti village. He was martyred at Galmaemot in 1866, and the two relatives recovered his body and buried it in this valley."

"Sapti is a pass between Sangcheon-ri in Hongsan-myeon and Geumji-ri in Naesan-myeon, Buyeo, a mountain refuge whose waters flow into the Sangcheon stream. Linked by mountain path to Father Choe Yang-eop's Doanggol, the road along which news passed between the two villages remains today as a walking pilgrimage section."

"In 1962 an earthenware jar holding a cross, a statue of Mary and a rosary was found at the presumed grave site and moved to the museum of Jeoldusan Martyrs' Shrine. Sapti Shrine has been restored since 2016, and near the excavation site a Hwang Seok-du memorial, an outdoor altar and a confession-of-faith stone have been placed."
