"Beomeo Cathedral was built as a project marking the centenary of the Archdiocese of Daegu and completed in 2016. Together with Gyesan Cathedral, built in 1902, it forms a two-cathedral system within one archdiocese."

"The cathedral provides a modern sanctuary for large-scale liturgies and spaces for diocesan events. The new cathedral in Beomeo-dong, Suseong-gu, shows a contemporary church architecture unlike the red-brick Gothic of Gyesan Cathedral."

"Where Gyesan Cathedral conveys the formation of Daegu's modern Church, Beomeo Cathedral is the liturgical and pastoral center of the Archdiocese of Daegu in the twenty-first century. With a bishop's chair in each, the two cathedrals share the roles of historic center and present-day diocesan function."
