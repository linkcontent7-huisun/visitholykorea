"The Seokjeong Boreum Well in Gye-dong, Bukchon, took its name from the tradition that its water ran clear for fifteen days and cloudy for fifteen. Before Seoul had a water supply, residents drew their household water from this stone well, now a Catholic historic site."

"Father James Zhou Wenmo, who crossed the Yalu River in 1794, hid in the nearby house of Choe In-gil. It is said that Father Zhou and Columba Kang Wan-suk used water from this well to baptize new believers in secret."

"Tradition also holds that Father Andrew Kim Taegon, returning home in 1845, stayed in Bukchon and used this water as holy water. The small well is a historic site on the Seoul pilgrimage route, linking Father Zhou's first ministry and Father Kim's work."
