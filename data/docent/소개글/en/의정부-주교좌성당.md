"Uijeongbu parish was founded in 1945 when Father Peter Kim moved the Deokjeong-ri parish here. After the wooden church burned in the Korean War, a stone church was rebuilt in 1953 with the help of Father Rojeski, a U.S. Army chaplain."

"To reduce damage from shelling during the war, the building was designed low and sturdy, its walls laid with local stone. This postwar stone church was designated Cultural Heritage Material No. 99 of Gyeonggi-do in 2002."

"Having sent out many daughter parishes, it was called the mother parish of the Uijeongbu area, and when the Diocese of Uijeongbu was established in 2004 it became the cathedral. The old church and the diocesan offices together convey the parish's rebuilding and the launch of the new diocese."
