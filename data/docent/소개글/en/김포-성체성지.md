"Gimpo Eucharistic Shrine is a pilgrimage site created by preserving, rather than demolishing, the old church building once used by Gimpo parish. A space where the parish community worshipped changed its role to become a shrine honoring the Eucharistic devotion of the Diocese of Incheon."

"Inside the old church, the altar and tabernacle for Mass and Eucharistic adoration remain at the center. The former building, standing beside the new church, shows the growth of Gimpo parish and the changes in its liturgical space."

"The Diocese of Incheon designated this a Eucharistic pilgrimage site so that the faithful may take part in adoration and prayer. Unlike a martyr's grave or an execution ground, it is a shrine of Eucharistic devotion that preserves a parish's old church itself."
