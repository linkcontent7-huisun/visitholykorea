"In 1845 Father Andrew Kim Taegon was ordained in Shanghai and returned home aboard the Raphael with Bishop Ferréol and Father Daveluy. After drifting ashore at Yongsu-ri on Jeju, the party landed at Nabawi on the Geum River at Hwangsanpo."

"Nabawi Church was begun by Father Vermorel in 1906 and completed in 1907. Combining a hanok hip-and-gable roof and wooden columns with a Western bell tower and arches, the building is National Historic Site No. 318."

"On the summit of Hwasan behind the church stand a marker commemorating the landing of Father Kim's party and a monument for the centenary of his martyrdom. The pilgrimage path linking the Geum River landing and the church follows the footsteps of the first Korean priest as he began his ministry in his homeland."
