"Gwandeokjeong in Jeju, built in 1448 for military training, is the oldest building on the island. The wide square in front of the Jeju provincial office was the public space of the old city, where troops assembled and trials and executions were held."

"During the Jeju uprising of 1901, the civilian militia led by Yi Jae-su gathered in the Gwandeokjeong square before advancing on Jeju Fortress. In a clash where tax disputes and hostility toward Catholicism overlapped, hundreds of believers and residents lost their lives."

"After the affair was settled, those held responsible were executed in front of Gwandeokjeong, and the square became a martyrdom site remembering both the conflict and the deaths. Touring the pavilion and the provincial office today, one can study the place where Church and local society collided."
