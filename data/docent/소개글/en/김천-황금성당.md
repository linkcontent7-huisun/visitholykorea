"In 1801 Bak Chun-san was exiled to Gimsan, the old name of Gimcheon, for the crime of believing in Catholicism, and Hyeon Gye-tak was exiled to Jeungsan for concealing his brother's faith. Believers who fled the persecutions that followed formed villages of faith at Majam, Meokbangi and Jangjateo."

"At Jarabatgol in Gimcheon, a hub of transport, Father Kim Seong-hak bought a thatched house and opened the parish in 1901. Following Gyesan and Gasil, it became the third parish of the Archdiocese of Daegu and grew into a base for mission in northern Gyeongsang."

"Across from the old red-brick Gothic church built in 1934 stands the centenary church of 2000. The new church was consecrated by Cardinal Kim Sou-hwan, and its interior furnishings are the work of Father Cho Kwang-ho, so that church architecture of two eras can be seen in one place."
