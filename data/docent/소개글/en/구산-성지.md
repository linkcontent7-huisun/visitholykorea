"Gusan village in Hanam is where St. Anthony Kim Seong-u was born and led a community of faith. Arrested in the Gihae Persecution of 1839, he was martyred in prison the following year, and in 1984 he was numbered among the 103 Korean Martyr Saints."

"The grave of St. Kim Seong-u was tended for generations by the village believers before the site was consecrated, and a memorial church honoring his life stands at the shrine. The name Gusan comes from the nine peaks that surround the village."

"Gusan is a rare village shrine where the faith of the believers' community continued unbroken even after the persecutions. The pilgrimage route laid out around the saint's remains and the site of his birthplace shows a faith kept together by one family and one village."
