"After the Eulsa incident of 1785, when the faith meeting at Myeongnyebang was discovered, Yi Byeok and Kim Beom-u, Jeong Yak-jeon and Jeong Yak-yong, Yi Seung-hun and Kwon Il-sin were taken to the Hyeongjo. Later, figures of the early Church such as Blessed Yu Hang-geom and Saints Kim Hyo-im and Kim Hyo-ju were also interrogated here."

"The Hyeongjo was the office in charge of punishment and judicial administration among the six ministries established with the founding of Joseon in 1392, and the site where the martyrs were interrogated is on the Sejong-daero pavement in front of today's Sejong Center for the Performing Arts. Walking from Gwanghwamun Station toward the main gate of Sejongno Park, a marker set in the ground indicates the location of the old office."

"No office buildings survive, but the ground marker and the Seoul Catholic pilgrimage route, approved by the Holy See in 2018, carry on the Hyeongjo site. On this route one also meets the nearby sites of the Uigeumbu, the Jeonokseo and the Right Police Bureau, and can follow the path by which believers passed through interrogation and imprisonment to the execution ground."
