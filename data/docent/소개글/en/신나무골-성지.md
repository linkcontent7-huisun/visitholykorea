"At Sinnamugol, believers who fled the Eulhae Persecution of 1815 formed a village of faith. Elizabeth Yi Seon-i, who lived here, was caught and martyred while fleeing to Hanti during the Gyeongsin Persecution, and her remains, once on her clan's ancestral hill, were moved to the shrine in 1984."

"Named after the maple trees that covered its valley, the village lay close to Daegu and became an outpost for missionaries. The moment Father Robert built a rectory and settled here in December 1885 is regarded as the beginning of Daegu's first parish, today's Gyesan parish."

"The shrine holds a bust of Father Robert, the thatched rectory where four missionaries stayed, and a monument to the cradle of Catholicism in Daegu. The cross-shaped hanok church of Gyesan-dong, lost to fire in 1901, was also reconstructed here and consecrated in May 2019."
