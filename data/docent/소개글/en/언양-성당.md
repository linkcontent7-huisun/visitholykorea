"The community of faith in Eonyang descends from the Bulldanggol mission station at Naeganwol, the first in Gyeongsangnam-do, and in 1926 it became the second parish of the Diocese of Busan. It is the central parish of the villages of believers in western Ulsan that endured the Byeongin Persecution."

"Designed by Father Beaudevin himself, the church was begun in 1929 and completed in 1936. Built with walls of local granite, pointed arches and a bell tower, it is the only stone Gothic church in the Diocese of Busan."

"The parish cares for thirteen shrines and historic sites and sixteen mission stations around it, and has produced more than seventy priests and religious. The church's exhibition space preserves prayer books and catechisms of the early believers, vestments, and records of parish groups."
