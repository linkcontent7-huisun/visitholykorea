"In the winter of 1784, under the leadership of Yi Byeok, Yi Seung-hun, the brothers Jeong Yak-jeon and Jeong Yak-yong, Kwon Il-sin and others held regular meetings of faith. Because laypeople began baptism and community on their own before any foreign missionary arrived, this gathering is regarded as the official founding of the Korean Catholic Church."

"The founding site is in the old Myeongnyebang, in the Myeong-dong area of Jung-gu, Seoul, and is handed down as the site of Yi Byeok's house near the Supyo Bridge. Early believers also met at the nearby house of Kim Beom-u, but this is a separate pilgrimage point remembering the first baptism and the founding event of 1784."

"Yi Byeok's house and the lanes of Myeongnyebang do not survive, so a marker in the city center identifies the founding place. The Seoul pilgrimage route from Myeongdong Cathedral past Kim Beom-u's house site and the founding site to the police bureaus shows in turn the birth of the community and its discovery in 1785."
