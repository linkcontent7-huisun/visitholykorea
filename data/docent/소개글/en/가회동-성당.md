"Father James Zhou Wenmo was the first foreign priest to enter Joseon, in 1794. On 5 April 1795, Easter Sunday, he celebrated the first Mass of the Korean Church at the house of Choe In-gil in Bukchon."

"Father Zhou then hid in the home of the woman leader Columba Kang Wan-suk, administering the sacraments and caring for the community. When he was denounced and hunted, Choe In-gil, Yun Yu-il and Ji Hwang helped him escape, were arrested in his place, and were martyred in the Eulmyo Persecution."

"Gahoe-dong parish was founded in 1949, and a new church combining a hanok with a modern sanctuary was consecrated in 2014. A history gallery on the ground floor and a rooftop sky garden present the memory of the first Mass together with the hanok landscape of Bukchon."
