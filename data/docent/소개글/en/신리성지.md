"Sinri is the village of believers where Bishop Daveluy recorded the history of the Joseon martyrs and published church books. He was arrested at nearby Geodeori in 1866 and martyred at Galmaemot; the bishop's house, also the birthplace of Thomas Son Ja-seon, preserves the last letter he wrote to his parents the day before his arrest."

"Set in the broad Naepo plain of Hapdeok, Dangjin, Sinri served as the diocesan headquarters during the persecutions. Bishop Daveluy and Luke Hwang Seok-du wrote catechisms here, and seven volumes of martyrdom records passed through Hong Kong to Paris, becoming the basis of Dallet's History of the Church of Korea."

"The birthplace built in 1815 was restored in 2004 as the thatched bishop's house. The Daveluy and Son Ja-seon Memorial Church was completed in 2006, and in 2017 a martyrs' art gallery opened beneath the memorial hall, displaying thirteen historical paintings by Lee Jong-sang and portraits of the five saints from Sinri."
