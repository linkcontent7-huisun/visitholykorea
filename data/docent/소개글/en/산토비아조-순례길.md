"Santo Viaggio is a walking pilgrimage route by which the Diocese of Jeju linked the traces of faith scattered across the island. The Kim Taegon, Kim Gi-ryang and Jeong Nan-ju trails bear the names of people bound to Jeju. Walking also the Hanon Church, Reconciliation and Isidore trails, one meets the history of the Jeju Church: its beginnings, exile, conflict and reconciliation."

"Each course, spread across Jeju, has a different starting point and scenery. The Kim Taegon Trail links Gosan Church, Yongsu Shrine and Sinchang Church, and the Hanon Church Trail passes Seogwipo Church, Hanon Shrine and the House of Myeonhyeong. The Jeong Nan-ju Trail joins Daejeong Shrine and Moseulpo Church, leading to the faith heritage of Seogwipo and western Jeju respectively."

"The markers met along the way hold concrete scenes of Jeju's history of faith. At Yongsu Shrine stands the church commemorating Father Kim Taegon's landing on Jeju, and at Daejeong Shrine lies the grave of Mary Jeong Nan-ju. The martyrs' memorial hall on the Kim Gi-ryang Trail and the exhibition hall on the Isidore Trail are also bases that tell the stories of each figure and community."
