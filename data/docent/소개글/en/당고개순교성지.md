"As the Gihae Persecution drew to a close in 1840, merchants of Seosomun asked that executions be halted before the New Year market, and the execution ground was moved to Danggogae. On 31 January and 1 February ten people were martyred; nine became saints, and Mary Yi Seong-rye was later declared blessed."

"The shrine is in Singye-dong, Yongsan, Seoul, once a marketplace on the banks of the Mancho stream along Wonhyo-ro, outside the capital's walls. Set between Saenamteo and Seosomun, it was the last martyrdom site of the Gihae Persecution and remembers the lives of martyrs who included three mothers of young children."

"The present church is designed on the theme of a warm mother's embrace, with a gallery of sacred images portraying the nine saints and one blessed. Established as a quasi-parish in 2012 and served by the Sisters of the Korean Blessed Martyrs, the shrine holds Masses for pilgrims and for honoring the martyrs without parish registration."
