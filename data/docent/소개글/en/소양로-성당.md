"Soyang-ro parish was separated from Jungnim-dong parish on 5 January 1950 and received Father Anthony Collier as its first pastor. When the Korean War broke out, Father Collier sent the believers to safety and stayed behind, and on 27 June he was martyred together with his altar server Gabriel Kim."

"The church stands on Soyang-ro in Chuncheon, Gangwon-do, at the southern foot of Bonguisan. Though the war delayed construction, Father James Buckley of the Missionary Society of St. Columban built the church in memory of his colleague Father Collier, and in 1956 it became the center of the community."

"Soyang-ro Church is an early modern church in Korea with a semicircular plan in which the pews fan out toward the central altar. The porch and confessional are at the center of the curve, with the sacristy and a nursing room at either end, and together with the statue of the Sacred Heart on the roof it is designated National Registered Cultural Heritage No. 161."
