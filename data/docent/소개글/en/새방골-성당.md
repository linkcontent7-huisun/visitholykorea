"Believers lived at Saebanggol from around the Gyeongsin Persecution of 1860, and a larger village of believers formed after the Byeongin Persecution. Father Robert, the itinerant missionary for Gyeongsang, moved here from Sinnamugol at the end of 1888 and, in hiding, prepared the mission to Daegu."

"When a fire next door spread in 1889, the wind suddenly changed and spared the house that held Father Robert's lodging and the church. The story that villagers who witnessed this came to learn the catechism survives in the letter Father Robert wrote on 3 April that year."

"Saebanggol is the site from which Father Robert later moved to the Namsan mission station, the forerunner of Gyesan Cathedral. In 2008 the community raised a bust of Father Robert for its hundred-and-twentieth anniversary, and in 2022 the church was renovated and a Mass of blessing was offered."
