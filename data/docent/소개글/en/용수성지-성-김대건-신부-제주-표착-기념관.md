"Father Andrew Kim Taegon, ordained in Shanghai in 1845, sailed home aboard the Raphael carrying Bishop Ferréol and Father Daveluy. Caught in a storm, the boat drifted ashore at the harbor of Yongsu-ri on the west coast of Jeju, and there the party celebrated their first Mass on Korean soil."

"In 1999 the Diocese of Jeju declared the Yongsu-ri coast a shrine and built the memorial church of the landing and a memorial hall. On the grounds stands a reconstruction of the Raphael, restored under expert supervision, showing the voyage and the size of the boat at the time."

"The second floor of the memorial hall holds the Father Kim Taegon gallery, the Jeju church-history gallery and a gallery for the deceased priests of the diocese. The shrine is also the central point of the 11.5-kilometer Kim Taegon Trail from Gosan Church past Suwolbong and Yongsu Shrine to Sinchang Church."
