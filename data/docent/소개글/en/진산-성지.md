"Jinsan is where Paul Yun Ji-chung and James Kwon Sang-yeon were arrested for destroying their ancestral tablets and holding a funeral according to Catholic rites. The Jinsan incident of 1791 led to the Sinhae Persecution, and the two were beheaded at Jeonju. Remembered as the first martyrs of the Korean Catholic Church, they were declared blessed in 2014."

"The shrine in Jibang-ri, Jinsan-myeon, Geumsan, honors the district where the two martyrs were born and raised. After the Jinsan incident the whole district was punished and demoted from a county to a lesser prefecture. The old Jibang-ri station continued as the shrine church, and the new church was also built in the same Jibang-ri, linking the old worship space with the martyrs' memorial space."

"The old Jinsan Church is a wooden building of 1927, in which two rows of columns beneath a gabled roof divide the central space from the side aisles. In the new church dedicated in 2023 are enshrined the remains of Yun Ji-chung, Kwon Sang-yeon and Yun Ji-heon. The timber structure of the small old church and the new memorial church show Jinsan in two different eras."
