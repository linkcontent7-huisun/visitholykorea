"Daegu's first Western-style Gothic brick church, completed in 1902 (Historic Site No. 290). This history preserves the faith of the community remembered here."

"The site stands at 10 Seoseong-ro, Jung-gu, Daegu (Gyesan-dong). Its roads and surrounding landscape help explain why the community formed here."

"5-kilometer walking pilgrimage from Gyesan Cathedral past the Gwandeokjeong Martyrs' Memorial and St. The 1. Its architecture gives visible form to the story."
