"Gyesan Cathedral grew from the Daegu parish, founded in the aftermath of persecution. Father Robert first cared for the faithful at Sinnamugol before moving into the town of Daegu. When the first hanok church was lost to fire, the community built again, and in 1911 the church became the cathedral leading the Church in Daegu."

"The church stands on level ground in Gyesan-dong, in Daegu's Jung-gu district. The hill at Dongsan-dong was also considered, but after debate the community chose the town center over an open field. Unlike a church looking down on the city from a height, this one made its home close to the lanes where people lived."

"The brick church completed in 1902 later gained a taller bell tower and a wider body, taking on its present form. Two spires and a rose window catch the eye, and stained glass made in France survives inside. The saints and colors behind the altar are the trace of a distant craft reaching a place of prayer in Daegu."
