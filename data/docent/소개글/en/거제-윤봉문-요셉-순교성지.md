"Joseph Yun Bong-mun, called the apostle of Geoje, was arrested in 1888 while spreading the faith among the island villages. Taken to Jinju, he refused to renounce his faith and was martyred; in 2014 he was beatified at the Gwanghwamun ceremony led by Pope Francis."

"The shrine lies on a mountain overlooking the sea at Jisepo, Irun-myeon, Geoje, Yun Bong-mun's hometown. A forest path of cypress and moso bamboo climbs from the old mountain grave to the shrine where his remains rest, showing the sea of Geoje and the martyr's home together."

"When the remains were moved to a new grave in 2013, a memorial tower, a Way of the Cross, a rosary path and a lookout were laid out. Yun Bong-mun's grave and the memorial facilities at the end of the cypress-forest prayer path turned the once cramped old grave into a pilgrimage space for the Diocese of Masan."
