"The Left Police Bureau, responsible for the eastern, central and southern parts of Seoul, patrolled the capital and arrested and interrogated believers. It hunted Father James Zhou Wenmo in 1801, and as the persecutions continued, Leo Yi Byeong-gyo, Paul Kim Deok-bin, Isidore Yi Yong-heon and others died in its prison."

"The martyrdom site is in Myo-dong, Jongno-gu, Seoul, around Donhwamun-ro on the site of the former Dansungsa theater. While the Right Police Bureau covered the western and northern districts toward Gwanghwamun, the Left Police Bureau had jurisdiction over the area east of Jongno, so the two offices formed the twin axes of the hunt for believers during the persecutions."

"The old office building does not survive, but a marker on the streets of Jongno and the police-bureau pilgrimage route carry on the site. In the martyrs' memorial hall beneath nearby Jongno Church, instruments and records of the police bureaus are displayed, so the interior of the vanished Left Police Bureau can be studied through its materials."
