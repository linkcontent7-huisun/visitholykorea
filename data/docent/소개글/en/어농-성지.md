"Paul Yun Yu-il traveled to Beijing three times and in 1794 brought Father James Zhou Wenmo to Joseon. The following year he was arrested together with Sabas Ji Hwang and Matthias Choe In-gil and martyred on 28 June; the three are remembered as those who protected Joseon's first foreign priest."

"Eonong-ri in Moga-myeon, Icheon, gathers the graves of Yun Yu-il's family and of martyrs of the Eulmyo and Sinyu Persecutions. The shrine was created in 1987 around the scattered remains and graves, and in 2002 the Diocese of Suwon designated it a shrine honoring the martyrs."

"A statue of Paul Yun Yu-il stands at the shrine entrance, and the martyrs, including his family, rest in the graves within. The memorial space for the seventeen beatified in 2014 and the youth training facilities link the history of bringing Father Zhou to Korea with the next generation."
