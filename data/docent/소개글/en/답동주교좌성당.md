"Dapdong Cathedral began as a parish founded in 1889, after the opening of the port of Jemulpo. Believers who had lived scattered through the persecutions joined this community, and the church grew into the base of mission in the Incheon area. With the sisters' childcare, free clinics and schooling, it became a place that cared for its neighbors' lives."

"The cathedral stands on the hill of Dapdong in Incheon's Jung-gu district. The Church chose this spot foreseeing the growth of the treaty port of Jemulpo. Today the square in front of the church connects to the Sinpo Market area. Walking between the old church of a harbor city and its market, you meet a scene where religion and daily life sit side by side."

"Building on the church consecrated in 1897, the outer walls were extended to reach the present form in 1937. It is Romanesque in style, with granite set among red brick and three bell towers on the façade. Rounded arches and windows work together, and the stained glass inside carries the colors of a rose and of the Passion and Resurrection of Jesus."
