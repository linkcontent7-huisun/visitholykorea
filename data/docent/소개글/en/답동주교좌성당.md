"One of Korea's oldest Western-style churches, built in 1889 with the opening of Jemulpo (Historic Site No. 287). Pilgrims still encounter that witness today."

"The site stands at 2 Uhyeon-ro 50beon-gil, Jung-gu, Incheon. 287. The exotic townscape of the old open port and a storied Catholic history meet in one place."

"Stand before the church and Incheon harbor spreads below, calling up the atmosphere of the port-opening years. Its architecture gives visible form to the story."
