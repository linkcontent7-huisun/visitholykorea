"During the Jeju uprising of 1901, about three hundred Catholics who had taken refuge in Jeju Fortress were killed. After negotiations between the Church and the Korean Empire over a burial ground, Hwangsapyeong was designated a Catholic cemetery in 1903, and in 1904 twenty-eight unclaimed bodies were moved here."

"Hwangsapyeong Shrine is a cemetery on a broad plain along Giwao-gil in Jeju City. The victims temporarily buried between Byeoldobong and the Hwabuk stream were moved here, and with the graves of ordinary believers and a columbarium it became the memorial ground of the Jeju Church."

"Among the crosses and gravestones of the plain stand a memorial to Peter Kim Gi-ryang, Jeju's first martyr, the grave of the first bishop of the diocese and monuments to foreign missionaries. The cemetery park, renovated for the centenary of the Jeju mission, became the main stage of the martyrs' commemoration begun in 1977."
