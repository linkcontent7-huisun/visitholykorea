"The Sisters of the Korean Blessed Martyrs built a memorial space at Oryundae to preserve the faith of the Busan martyrs. In 1977 they moved the graves of eight martyrs, including John Yi Jeong-sik and Martin Yang Jae-hyeon, from Myeongjang-dong, and consecrated the martyrs' church the same year."

"The museum stands beside the convent and the martyrs' graves on Oryundae-ro in Geumjeong-gu, Busan. The Korean Martyrs' Memorial Hall, opened in 1982, collected church relics together with folk objects of the late Joseon era, and in 2009 it was raised to the Oryundae Korean Martyrs' Museum."

"The three exhibition floors are divided among church-history materials and instruments of torture, court artifacts and ceramics. Outside, the martyrs' church, the eight graves, the Way of the Cross and the Marian grotto connect the relics inside the museum with the actual martyrdom sites of Busan in one space."
