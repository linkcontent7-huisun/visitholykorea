"The Right Police Bureau, with jurisdiction over the western and northern parts of Seoul, was the office that arrested and interrogated believers. It is where Father Andrew Kim Taegon wrote his last letter from prison before his martyrdom, and where believers including Peter Yu Dae-cheol were martyred after torture and imprisonment."

"The martyrdom site is in Cheongjin-dong, Jongno-gu, Seoul, between the Gwanghwamun post office and the Ilmin Museum of Art. Included in the Seoul pilgrimage route approved by the Holy See in 2018, this spot on the road from Gwanghwamun to the crossroads outside Seosomun shows the judicial path by which believers, after arrest and interrogation, were taken to the execution ground."

"The bureau building has vanished, but a marker and the pilgrimage route remember the place. The Way of Life on the Seoul Catholic pilgrimage route sets the Right Police Bureau site in line with the Hyeongjo site, the Gyeonggi Gamyeong site and the Seosomun martyrs' shrine, so that the offices and execution grounds of the persecution years can be read in a single line."
