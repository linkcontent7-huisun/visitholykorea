"Thomas Choe Yang-eop, Korea's second native priest, was ordained in Shanghai in 1849 and for nearly twelve years walked among the villages of believers in five provinces. Setting out to make his pastoral report, he fell ill at Jinan-ri in Mungyeong in 1861 and died at forty."

"Jinan-ri lies below the Joryeong gate of Mungyeong Saejae, the pass linking Yeongnam and Seoul. It was the mission road Father Choe walked between the villages of Chungcheong and Gyeongsang and the way toward the capital, so his last journey overlaps with this mountain path."

"Jinan-ri Shrine holds a memorial space marking where Father Choe fell ill, and a pilgrimage path. The road toward Joryeong and the small memorial facilities recall the priest of sweat who, after studying in Macau and being ordained in Shanghai, walked without rest."
