"Gasil Church grew from a mission base that cared for believers along the Nakdong River into the oldest parish in Gyeongsangbuk-do. Missionaries and local faithful traveled to distant mission stations to build the community, and kept the faith here through war and upheaval."

"The church stands in a village of Waegwan, Chilgok, where the Nakdong River meets the fields. Close to the road people traveled along the river, it became a hub linking mission stations across the region, and its bell tower rising above the low farmland long served as a landmark for the village."

"Built of red brick, the church shows a neo-Romanesque style of continuous rounded arches. Church and old rectory are preserved together, and inside a statue of St. Anne and stained glass depicting the life of Jesus make the building and its sacred art one story."
