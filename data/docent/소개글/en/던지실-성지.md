"The roots of the Deonjisil community lie in the Seonbawi mission station at Osan-ri, Geumgwang-myeon, Anseong. Cared for at first by Gongseri parish, it came under Gupo-dong parish in 1900. Long continued as a station, the community became Deonjisil parish in 1992, with Father Fleming of the Maryknoll Society as its first pastor."

"Deonjisil is the old name of this district, and the church stands in Gaesan-ri, Geumgwang-myeon, Anseong. Seonbawi in Osan-ri, where the faith began, and Gaesan-ri, where the church was built, are different villages. When the parish was founded it also cared for nearby stations such as Sangjung-ri, Jangjuk-ri and Seonbawi, linking the farming communities of Geumgwang-myeon."

"To raise money for the building, the believers planted pumpkins in an abandoned orchard they had rented. They loaded the harvest into a van and sold it from parish to parish in Seoul. Thanks to the farming and fundraising carried on by Father Fleming and the faithful, the church begun in 1992 was dedicated in 1995."
