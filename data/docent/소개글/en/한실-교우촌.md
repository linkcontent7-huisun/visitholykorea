"The Hansil village of believers began in the Sinyu Persecution of 1801, when the family of Seo Yu-do fled to Jatgol in Mungyeong. Around the Byeongin Persecution it became a pastoral base for Father Calais, but in 1866 some fifteen believers were captured and martyred."

"Hansil in Sangnae-ri, Maseong-myeon, Mungyeong, was a natural fortress enclosed by the valleys of Baekhwasan. Father Calais moved from here between the villages of Mungyeong and Yeonpung, and the mountain paths served both as hiding places and as mission roads."

"At the village site remain the old house sites and markers honoring the martyrs' names. The records of the catechist brothers Kim Ye-gi and Kim In-gi, executed together in Daegu in 1867, and of the fifteen Hansil believers martyred at the Sangju office made this valley a shrine."
