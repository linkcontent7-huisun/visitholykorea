"The Menemok village of believers in the mountains of Boeun first appears in records of the Jeonghae Persecution of 1827. Paul Bak Gyeong-hwa and his son Andrew Bak Sa-ui lived here, forming a community of faith."

"Father Thomas Choe Yang-eop crossed rugged mountain paths to visit the believers of Menemok and administer the sacraments. Three people connected with this village — Bak Gyeong-hwa, Bak Sa-ui and a Mrs. Kim — were beatified by Pope Francis in 2014."

"The Diocese of Cheongju surveyed the long-forgotten village site and designated it an official pilgrimage shrine in 2016. The old living site and prayer space, ringed by mountains, convey the scale of a family community that lived in hiding from persecution."
