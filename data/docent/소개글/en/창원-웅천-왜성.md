"The Jesuit Father Gregorio de Céspedes came to the Japanese fortress at Ungcheon in 1593 at the request of the Catholic general Konishi Yukinaga. For about a year he celebrated Mass and baptized among the Japanese troops, and is recorded as the first Western priest to stay on Korean soil."

"Ungcheon Waeseong is a military stronghold the Japanese army built overlooking Jinhae Bay during the Imjin War. Because the first stay and Mass of a Spanish priest took place inside an invading army's camp, the Catholic memory of this place holds together the wounds of war and the record of mission."

"Below the stone ramparts and the hilltop remains, Céspedes Park has been laid out in Nammun-dong. The city of Changwon opened a space commemorating the priest's arrival in 2015, and the faithful of the Diocese of Masan have celebrated Mass at the fortress to recall the record of 1593."
