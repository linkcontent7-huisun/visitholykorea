"Jamdubong on the Han River came to be called Jeoldusan — beheading hill — after believers were beheaded here during the Byeongin Persecution of 1866. The persecution unfolded here after the French fleet sailed up to Yanghwajin, and not only those whose names are known but nameless martyrs too lost their lives."

"Jeoldusan is a rocky hill in Mapo, Seoul, overlooking the Yanghwa ferry landing. Unlike the old meaning of Yanghwajin — the landing where willow blossoms bloom — the memory of martyrdom was carved here, and in 2018 it was included in the Way of Unity of the Seoul pilgrimage routes approved by the Holy See."

"For the centenary of the Byeongin martyrdom, construction of the memorial church and museum began in 1965. The crypt of the church holds twenty-seven of the 103 saints and one nameless martyr, and in the yard stands the statue of Father Kim Taegon blessed in 1972."
