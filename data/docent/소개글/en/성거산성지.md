"On Seonggeosan, seven villages of believers followed one another from the 1860s to the 1920s, and during the Byeongin Persecution twenty-three people from six of those villages were martyred. The family of Father Choe Yang-eop's uncle also lived at Seodeokgol, forming one pillar of the mountain community of faith."

"Set in the mountains of Ipjang-myeon, Cheonan, at about five hundred meters above sea level, Seonggeosan was a refuge well hidden from the eyes of the authorities. The village sites scattered along the Wirye mountain path, such as Sohakgol and Seodeokgol, were living spaces where hillside farming and faith were kept together."

"At the Sohakgol village site, three thatched houses have been restored to show mountain life in the years of persecution. The small chapel of Seonggeosan Shrine, the martyrs' graves and the prayer spaces along the mountain path link the traces of the seven villages into one pilgrimage route."
