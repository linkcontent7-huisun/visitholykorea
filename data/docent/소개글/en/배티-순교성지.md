"A secret Catholic village community formed from around 1830, known as the catacombs of Korean Catholicism. Pilgrims still encounter that witness today."

"The site stands at Yangbaek-ri, Baekgok-myeon, Jincheon-gun, Chungcheongbuk-do. Its roads and surrounding landscape help explain why the community formed here."

"Twenty-nine of them are known by name; the other twenty-six lie in graves with no name on them. Its architecture gives visible form to the story."
