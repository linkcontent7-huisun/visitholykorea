"The Nammun Market area of Seongdong-dong, Sangju, is the site of the prison of the Sangju garrison in the late Joseon era. During the Jeonghae Persecution of 1827, believers arrested in several villages around Sangju were held here for interrogation and punishment."

"Bak Bo-rok of Meongaemok and his son Bak Sa-ui, Andrew Sa-geon of Aengmu-dong, and the family of Sin Tae-bo of Jatgol all passed through this prison. It shows how arrests carried out across different villages of believers converged on the jail of the Sangju office."

"The old prison no longer survives, and the lanes of Nammun Market now occupy its site. A martyrdom-site marker inside the market leaves in today's city center the last trace of the believers who awaited trial during the Jeonghae Persecution."
