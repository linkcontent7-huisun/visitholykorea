"The bodies of Bishop Daveluy, Fathers Aumaître and Huin, and Joseph Jang Ju-gi, martyred at Galmaemot in 1866, were recovered at the risk of their lives by the believers of Seojitgol. Those who buried the four martyrs were themselves arrested soon after and executed in Seoul."

"The grave at Seojitgol in Misan-myeon, Boryeong, is where the martyrs' remains rested for fifteen years and six months. In 1882 they were moved to Oura Church in Nagasaki, Japan, and after passing through the Yongsan seminary and Myeongdong Cathedral were laid to rest at Jeoldusan in 1967."

"At the shrine laid out in 2013 stands a black outdoor altar engraved with the names Daveluy, Aumaître, Huin and Jang Ju-gi. This altar of the four saints and the great cross, raised on a former tobacco field, remember together the temporary grave of the four saints and the believers of Seojitgol who guarded their remains."
