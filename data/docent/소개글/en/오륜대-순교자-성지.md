"At Oryundae lie the graves of eight martyrs of the Byeongin Persecution in Busan, including John Yi Jeong-sik and Martin Yang Jae-hyeon. In 1977 the Sisters of the Korean Blessed Martyrs moved their remains here from Myeongjang-dong and enshrined them together with the martyrs' church."

"On the hill of Oryundae in Geumjeong-gu, Busan, beyond the museum follow the graves, the Way of the Cross and the rosary garden. It is a layout where the graves of the Busan and Ulsan martyrs and the relics of Korean Church history meet within one precinct."

"Beneath the altar of the martyrs' church rest the relics of twenty-six of the 103 Korean Martyr Saints. A restored model of the Raphael inside the church shows Father Kim Taegon's homeward voyage, while the eight martyrs' graves outside carry on the history of persecution in the Busan area."
