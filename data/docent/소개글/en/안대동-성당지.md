"Around Andae-dong, Fathers Blanc and Doucet preached the gospel from 1878, and the station appears in the church statistics of 1896. Father Vermorel of Nabawi parish pursued a separation for the believers of Iksan and Impi, and on 19 October 1910 Andae-dong parish was founded."

"The church site is in the Andae-dong area of Iksan, Jeollabuk-do, and was the center where believers of Hamyeol and Yongan, far from Nabawi, received the sacraments. The first pastor, Father Seo Byeong-ik, took over eleven stations and 1,088 believers from Nabawi and used the Andae-dong station house as a temporary church."

"Andae-dong parish was merged into Nabawi parish in 1914 for lack of a priest and reopened in 1938. In 1951 Father Song Nam-ho reinforced the church walls with brick, but the parish later moved to Hamyeol, and today the site of the early church conveys these turns of fortune."
