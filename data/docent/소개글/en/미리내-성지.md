"Mirinae, in a deep valley of Anseong, took its name — the Milky Way — from the lights that believers fleeing persecution lit each night, which looked like a river of stars. In 1846 Vincent Yi Min-sik secretly carried the remains of St. Andrew Kim Taegon here and buried them."

"At Father Kim's grave lie also the graves of his mother Ursula Go and of Vincent Yi Min-sik, among others. St. Joseph's Church, built in 1906, is a registered national cultural heritage that conveys the architecture and religious life of an early village of believers."

"The Memorial Church of the Canonization of the 103 Korean Martyrs, consecrated in 1991, is the central space where large pilgrimage Masses are held. Seeing Father Kim's tomb and the old St. Joseph's Church together shows how a burial ground grew into a national shrine."
