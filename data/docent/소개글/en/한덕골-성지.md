"Handeokgol is the village of believers where the family of Father Kim Taegon stayed after leaving Solmoe to escape persecution. From here the family moved on to Golbaemasil. Relatives of Father Choe Yang-eop also lived at Handeokgol, and his orphaned youngest brother Choe Sin-jeong grew up here and was reunited with his brother on his return to Korea."

"Set in a mountain valley of Muk-ri, Idong-eup, Cheoin-gu, Yongin, Handeokgol lies between Eunyi Shrine and Mirinae Shrine. Originally the name covered not only the lower village but Gwangpari-gol on the mountainside. Matthias Kim Jin-yong rediscovered the fading traces of the village, secured the land and donated it to the diocese."

"Beside the outdoor altar at Handeokgol stand a large cross and stone statues of two priests. The statues of Father Kim Taegon and Father Choe Yang-eop stand side by side, revealing the bond of the two families tied to this valley. At the historic site tended by Cheolli Joseph parish, a statue of Our Lady of Fatima was also erected and blessed in 2010."
