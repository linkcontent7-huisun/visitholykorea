"John Nam Jong-sam, who held the office of royal secretary, was martyred outside Seosomun in the Byeongin Persecution of 1866. His father Nam Sang-gyo died at Gongju and his wife Yi Jo-i at her place of exile in Changnyeong. This graveyard holds together a family that suffered persecution in different places, though the grave of the eldest son Nam Myeong-hui, martyred at Jeonju, is not here."

"The graves are in the Uiryeong Nam family graveyard in Uldae-ri, Jangheung-myeon, Yangju. They are reached by following the marker stones up the mountain path from the entrance of the Uldae-ri cemetery of Gireum-dong Church. The youngest son Nam Gyu-hui, who lost his martyred father and brother and endured exile in Changnyeong, is also buried here with his wife."

"Before the grave of Nam Jong-sam stand side by side an old gravestone naming him blessed and a new one naming him saint. The new stone was erected in 2018. The graves of Nam Sang-gyo and Nam Gyu-hui also have their own stones, so one can read the names of family members of the same Nam house who walked the different paths of martyrdom and exile."
