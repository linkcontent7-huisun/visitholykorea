"Yeosaul is the village where Ludovico Yi Jon-chang, the apostle of Naepo, was born and preached the gospel. Taught the catechism by Kwon Il-sin, he also worked to bring Father Zhou Wenmo to Korea, and in the Sinyu Persecution of 1801 he was beheaded at Hwangsaebawi in Gongju, a martyr at forty-two."

"Yeosaul in Sinam-myeon, Yesan, lies in the Naepo plain between the Sapgyo and Muhan streams. Yi Jon-chang's house became a meeting place and lodging for the faithful, and his evangelizing reached the Kim family of Solmoe and the family of Father Choe Yang-eop, laying the foundation of the community of faith in Chungcheong."

"Excavation of the house site in 1984 uncovered a crucifix, a statue of Mary and a scapular medal that appear to have come from China. An outdoor altar and a Way of the Cross were placed at the site, and the Yi Jon-chang Martyr Memorial Church, roofed with Spanish-style tiles, was consecrated in 2010."
