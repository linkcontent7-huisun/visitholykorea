"Jemuljindu was the old ferry landing of Jemulpo in Incheon, where believers were publicly executed during the Byeongin Persecution. In 1868 four were martyred here, including Augustine Yi Yeon-gu and Paul Yi Gyun-gu, great-grandsons of Peter Yi Seung-hun."

"In 1845 St. Andrew Kim Taegon boarded a boat near this landing and sailed for Shanghai to be ordained. Two memories overlap here: a place of martyrdom, and the shore where the first Korean priest's voyage began."

"The Diocese of Incheon secured the site in 2011 and in 2014 consecrated a memorial chapel fifteen meters high. The chapel facing the sea and the martyrs' memorial mark the position of Jemuljindu, whose old waterway has receded through land reclamation."
