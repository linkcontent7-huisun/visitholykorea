"Chimyeongjasan holds the remains of Augustine Yu Hang-geom and his family, martyred in the Sinyu Persecution of 1801. Of the seven, including the virgin couple John Yu Jung-cheol and Lutgarda Yi Sun-i, five were beatified during Pope Francis's visit to Korea in 2014."

"Rising at the summit of Seungamsan east of the Jeonju Hanok Village, the mountain is called Chimyeongjasan, the mountain of martyrs. In 1914 Father Baudounet moved the remains of Yu Hang-geom's family, scattered around Chonami and Jeonju, to this high place."

"At the summit graveyard, the joint graves of Yu Hang-geom and his wife and of their children lie side by side. A Way of the Cross climbs from the foot of the mountain to the graves, and from the martyrs' memorial church and viewpoint at the top one can see the city of Jeonju and the family graves together."
