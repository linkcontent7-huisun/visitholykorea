"Yu Seom-i was the daughter of Augustine Yu Hang-geom, called the apostle of Honam. When her father was martyred at Jeonju in the Sinyu Persecution of 1801, nine-year-old Seom-i was made a government slave of Geoje as the child of a criminal, and she kept her faith without ever marrying."

"The daughter of the martyr Yu Hang-geom lies buried on a hillside at Naegan-ri, Geoje-myeon, the place of her exile. On Geoje, Yu Seom-i dug herself a cave dwelling and lived in prayer for twenty-five years, never returning to her hometown of Chonami before her death at about seventy-one."

"The small mound at Naegan-ri is cared for by Geoje parish and continues as a Korean Catholic pilgrimage site. Parishioners of Ujeon Church in Jeonju brought soil, water and turf from Chonami Shrine, where Yu Hang-geom's home stood, and laid them on the mound, joining the father's hometown to the daughter's place of exile."
