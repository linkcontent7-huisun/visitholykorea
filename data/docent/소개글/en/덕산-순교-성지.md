"In 1868 the German merchant Oppert attempted to dig up the tomb of Namyeongun on the slopes of Gayasan in Deoksan, an incident that hardened the Daewongun's suppression of Catholicism. In its wake, believers around Deoksan were also arrested and killed, and the shrine remembers these nameless martyrs."

"The shrine is in Sangga-ri, Deoksan-myeon, Yesan, Chungcheongnam-do, where the tomb of Namyeongun and Gayasan meet. Because the site of the grave-robbing incident and the memory of the Deoksan martyrs overlap in one area, the place makes it possible to understand through its landscape why the Byeongin Persecution intensified."

"Deoksan Martyrs' Shrine is one of the Jubilee 2025 pilgrimage sites designated by the Diocese of Daejeon. Rather than a grand old church, its center is the memorial space honoring the martyrs' remains and the events of Deoksan, and the graveyard scenery on the slopes of Gayasan forms the historical setting of the shrine."
