"The remains of Bishop Imbert and Fathers Maubant and Chastan, martyred at Saenamteo in the Gihae Persecution of 1839, were carried to Nogosan by the hands of believers. The three clergy lay buried here for four years before their remains were moved again."

"Nogosan is on the campus of Sogang University in Sinsu-dong, Mapo-gu, Seoul. Close to execution grounds such as Saenamteo, the hill was well suited for recovering and hiding the martyrs' bodies; today the pine grove in front of the Gabriel Hall of the Jesuit-run university is the shrine."

"On 15 June 2009 Sogang University raised a martyrs' memorial to the three clergy in front of Gabriel Hall, blessed by Cardinal Cheong Jin-suk. The central memorial and the stones honoring Imbert, Maubant and Chastan stand side by side beneath the pines."
