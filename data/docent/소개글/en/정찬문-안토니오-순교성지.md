"Anthony Jeong Chan-mun, born in Sabong-myeon, Jinju, joined the Church in 1863 at his wife's urging and was arrested while evangelizing during the Byeongin Persecution. He died after enduring twenty-five days of torture in the Jinju prison, while his wife, Mrs. Yun, carried food to the jail with their baby on her back."

"His cousins recovered only the body from the corpse left three days in the prison and buried it by the road at Heoyu Pass in his hometown. The grave, called the headless tomb, was identified again in 1948 through Father Seo Jeong-do of Munsan Church and the villagers of Muchon-ri."

"In 1975 the remains were moved to the martyrs' graveyard of the Sabong mission station, and in 1978 the grave and martyr's monument were renewed. Munsan Church built a new station in 2005 for its centenary, and the graveyard came to bear the name of Jeong Chan-mun, beatified in 2014."
