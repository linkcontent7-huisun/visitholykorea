"Where believers who had fled the Eulhae Persecution (1815) were seized in the Jeonghae Persecution of 1827, tortured in the converted county guesthouse."

"The site stands at Gokseong-eup, Gokseong-gun, Jeollanam-do. Its roads and surrounding landscape help explain why the community formed here."

"One hundred and thirty years later, in 1957, the Archdiocese of Gwangju founded the Gokseong parish on this ground; today the prison room."
