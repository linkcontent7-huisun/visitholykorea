"Bishop Demange, the first bishop of the diocese, vowed to build a Marian grotto once the bishop's residence, the seminary and the enlargement of the cathedral were achieved. When Father Sauzet recovered from a grave illness, the work was brought forward, and the grotto was consecrated on 13 October 1918."

"Inside the diocesan compound in Namsan-dong, Daegu, lies also the clergy cemetery laid out in 1915. Set on the Namsan Centenary Path linking the grotto and St. Justin's Seminary, it holds the graves of seven bishops, including Bishop Demange, and of many priests."

"The grotto copies the size and rock form of Lourdes in France, while its brick exterior follows the grotto Pope Leo XIII built in the Vatican. Latin inscriptions of 1911 and 1918 on the façade, and the words at the cemetery entrance — today for me, tomorrow for you — carve time into the place."
