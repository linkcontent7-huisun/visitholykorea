"Gangwon Gamyeong in Wonju was the administrative center where the governor of Gangwon Province conducted affairs for about five hundred years. During the persecutions, Catholics arrested across Gangwon were interrogated before Seonhwadang and martyred in the gamyeong's prison and execution ground."

"Simon Kim Gang-i, John Choe Hae-seong and Barbara Choe Yeong-i are the representative martyrs connected with Gangwon Gamyeong. They were among the 124 Korean martyrs beatified at the Mass Pope Francis celebrated at Gwanghwamun in 2014."

"Pojeongnu, the main gate of the gamyeong, and Seonhwadang, the governor's office, are Tangible Cultural Heritage No. 3 of Gangwon. A restoration project in 2018 reorganized the old office grounds, so the courtroom space and the history of martyrdom can be studied together on site."
