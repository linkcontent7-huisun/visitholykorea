"Nam Myeong-hui, son of John Nam Jong-sam, martyred at Saenamteo in the Byeongin Persecution, and the unnamed son of Thomas Hong Bong-ju had their executions postponed because they were not yet fifteen. In 1867 the two boys were pushed from Chorokbawi, the green rock, into the Jeonju stream and drowned."

"The martyrdom site is in Dongwansan-dong, Wansan-gu, Jeonju, Jeollabuk-do, between the Ssajeon Bridge and Seocheon Bridge on the Jeonju stream. In the Joseon era the water was deep enough for boats, but embankment works after the flood of 1936 cut away much of the rock and changed the old riverbank."

"Today a gray concrete retaining wall and a road surround the spot in place of the green rock. In 2006 the Diocese of Jeonju raised on the riverbank a mosaic memorial depicting the martyrdom of the two boys, painted by the artist Nam Yong-u, a descendant of Nam Jong-sam."
