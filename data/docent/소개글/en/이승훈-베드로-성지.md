"Peter Yi Seung-hun was baptized in Beijing in 1784 and returned to become Joseon's first baptized Christian. Leader of the lay community of faith, he was beheaded outside Seosomun in the Sinyu Persecution of 1801 and buried on his family's ancestral hill in Jangsu-dong, Incheon."

"At the end of the Yi Seung-hun Peter Trail, which runs from Mansu Station to Incheon Grand Park Station, lie the graves and a history park. The graves of Yi Seung-hun and his two sons remain on the ancestral hill, and the four tiers of the Pietà pond express the history of a family that gave eight martyrs across four generations."

"The memorial hall, opened in 2024, is a circular building of one underground and two upper floors. The tabernacle in the chapel is made of forty-five pieces of glass, the age at which Yi Seung-hun was martyred, and a 400-meter wooden path behind it carries the Way of the Cross to the graves."
