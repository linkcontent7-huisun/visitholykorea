"James Heo In-baek, Luke Kim Jong-ryun and Peter Yi Yang-deung were living their faith in the Beomgul cave on Danseoksan in Gyeongju when they were caught. Taken by way of Gyeongju to Ulsan, the three were beheaded at the garrison jangdae on 14 September 1868, and were declared blessed in 2014."

"The jangdae was the stone platform from which the military commander directed his troops, and Jangdaebeol means the field where that facility stood. This martyrdom site in Namoe-dong, Jung-gu, Ulsan, close to the garrison of Gyeongsang Left Province, established in Ulsan in 1417, and the Dongcheon stream, served both as a drill ground and as an execution site for serious offenders."

"The Diocese of Busan began building the martyrs' shrine church in 2013 and dedicated the two-story church in 2014. Outside the church an outdoor altar and plaza have been laid out, and the Dongcheon embankment path and the garrison field show the last road of the three blesseds in one space."
