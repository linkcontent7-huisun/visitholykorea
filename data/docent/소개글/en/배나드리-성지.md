"Baenadeuri is a martyrdom site in Yesan honoring Blessed Martin In Eon-min. Born in Yongdong-ri, he was arrested in the Jeongsa Persecution and martyred at Haemi. In 1817 constables also raided the Baenadeuri village, and several believers, including Peter Min Cheomji and his sister-in-law Anna, died in prison."

"The name Baenadeuri in Yongdong-ri, Sapgyo-eup, Yesan, carries the waters of the Sapgyo stream. When floods came the village was surrounded by water and could be reached only by boat, and as an island-like village it was also called Dori. The nearby Haryong hamlet of Yongdong-ri is the hometown of Blessed In Eon-min, where a historic site commemorates him."

"At the Baenadeuri site stand a memorial to Martin In Eon-min and a stone inscribed with the words Glory to the Martyrs. In the middle of a garden laid out in the shape of a cross rests an outdoor altar of natural stone. The name In Eon-min on the monument and the stone altar bring the life of a man martyred at Haemi back to his home village."
