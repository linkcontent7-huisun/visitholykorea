"Myeongnye, on the Nakdong River in Miryang, is where the first parish in the territory of the Diocese of Masan was founded in 1897. Father Laurence Kang Seong-sam, Korea's third native priest, served as its first pastor and died here in 1903."

"Blessed Mark Sin Seok-bok was born at Myeongnye in 1828 and made his living selling yeast and salt. Arrested in the Byeongin Persecution of 1866, he endured torture in Daegu and was martyred by hanging on 31 March."

"Myeongnye Church, rebuilt on a smaller scale in 1938, is designated Cultural Heritage Material No. 526 of Gyeongsangnam-do. In 2018 a memorial church was consecrated on the site of Sin Seok-bok's birthplace, conveying the beginnings of the parish and the life of the martyr together."
