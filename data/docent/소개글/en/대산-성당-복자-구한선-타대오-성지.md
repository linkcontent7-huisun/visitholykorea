"Thaddeus Gu Han-seon, from Minarigol in Haman, was baptized by Bishop Daveluy and helped Father Ridel's mission as far as Geoje as his server. Arrested in 1866, he was beaten while writing out the catechism for the magistrate's wife, and died seven days after his release."

"Gu Han-seon's grave was found in 1959 and moved to Gadeungsan in Pyeongnim-ri in 1976. Because of surrounding factories and a steep approach, the Diocese of Masan reinterred his remains within the grounds of Daesan Church in Haman in 2016 and created a pilgrimage space."

"The blessed's remains rest beneath the altar of the tomb chapel on one floor of Daesan Church. The church on the second floor and the outdoor altar hold part of the relics and a relief of Gu Han-seon, showing the faith and filial devotion of a sixteen-year-old martyr beatified in 2014."
