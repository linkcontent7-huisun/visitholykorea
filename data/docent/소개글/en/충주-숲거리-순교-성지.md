"Yi Gi-yeon, the apostle of Chungju, received the faith through Kwon Il-sin and passed it to family and relatives until he was arrested in the Sinyu Persecution and beheaded in 1802. Yi Bu-chun and Kwon A-gi-ryeon also kept their faith and were martyred at the Supgeori execution ground, and the beatification process for the three is under way."

"Supgeori is the area between Munhwa-dong and Bongbang-dong in Chungju, Chungcheongbuk-do, where woods once stretched from the old Muhakdang to the bank of the Chungju stream. It was Chungju's principal execution ground, close to the military training ground and command post, and the area behind today's Chungju post office has been confirmed as the martyrdom site."

"No buildings of the old execution ground survive, but the Diocese of Cheongju proclaimed the site a martyrs' shrine on 29 November 2020. The representative address on Gugwon-daero in Munhwa-dong and on-site markers indicate the widely scattered extent of Supgeori and honor the names of Yi Gi-yeon, Yi Bu-chun and Kwon A-gi-ryeon."
