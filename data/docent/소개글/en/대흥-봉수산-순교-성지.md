"Peter Kim Jeong-deuk and his cousin Andrew Kim Gwang-ok were arrested together in the Sinyu Persecution of 1801. Ordered to be executed in their separate hometowns, they parted with the promise to meet again in heaven, were martyred on the same day, and were beatified together in 2014."

"The shrine lies beside the old county hall and prison site below Bongsusan in Daeheung-myeon, Yesan, Chungcheongnam-do. At the end of the Brothers' Path, named for Yi Seong-man and Yi Sun, the brothers said to have secretly shared their rice sheaves, one meets another bond of affection: Kim Jeong-deuk and Kim Gwang-ok, who shared both blood and faith."

"In the yard stands the Hyeongokwon, a reconstruction of the Daeheung office prison where the martyrs were held, built with traditional timber, earthen walls and a tiled roof. Its small barred windows, three cells, and instruments such as the cangue and the paddle show the conditions of confinement, and a statue of Mary stands on a base modeled on the execution block."
