"Jangdae in Suyeong, Busan, was the military command post of the Gyeongsang Left Naval Command and the place where prisoners were publicly executed. In September 1868, eight believers, including the family of John Yi Jeong-sik and Martin Yang Jae-hyeon, were martyred by military beheading."

"Yi Jeong-sik, a former military officer, became a catechist at fifty-nine and led the community; his son Francis Yi Wol-ju and daughter-in-law Mary Bak Jo-i were arrested with him. Yi Jeong-sik and Yang Jae-hyeon were beatified by Pope Francis in 2014."

"After a martyrs' memorial was raised in 1988, a large cross, the fourteen Stations and memorial tablets for the martyrs were added in 2004. At the Jangdae site within the old naval fortress walls, the names of the Busan martyrs of the Byeongin Persecution can be read."
