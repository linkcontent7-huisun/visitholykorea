"Juksan in Anseong was a county town with a Joseon garrison office, where believers arrested during the persecutions were interrogated and executed. Twenty-four martyrs whose names are confirmed in records gave their lives at the execution grounds of this area."

"The execution site known as Ijinteo was long forgotten and left as farmland before being laid out as a shrine. At the old prison site stand monuments honoring the blessed couple Francis Bak Gyeong-jin and Margaret O."

"The shrine opened a spirituality center in 2013 and continues to hold retreats and Masses for pilgrims. The path linking the prison site of the Juksan office and Ijinteo retraces the martyrs' last steps from trial to execution."
