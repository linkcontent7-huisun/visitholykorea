"At Cheongju Fortress, believers lost their lives from the Sinyu Persecution of 1801 to the Byeongin Persecution of 1866. James Won Si-bo, Francis Bae Gwan-gyeom, Francis Kim Sa-jip, Paul O Ban-ji and Thomas Jang were beatified in 2014."

"The martyrdom sites are not one place but four scattered across the old city center: the Cheongju garrison, the prison site, the market outside the south gate and the military platform. The pilgrimage route starting from Seoun-dong Church links on foot the martyrdom sites and places of witness within the old fortress."

"Seoun-dong Church, founded in 1932, is the first parish of Cheongju and oversees the Cheongju Fortress martyrs' shrine. The church holds the remains of Blessed Paul O Ban-ji, and the urban pilgrimage guide points out the places where the five blesseds and three Servants of God passed."
