"Jeondong Cathedral was built on the site where Paul Yun Ji-chung and James Kwon Sang-yeon were martyred in the Sinhae Persecution of 1791. Refusing Confucian ancestral rites in obedience to the Church's teaching, the two became the first martyrs of the Korean Catholic Church. In 1801, Yu Hang-geom, who had spread the faith through Honam, and his companions also died here."

"The cathedral stands at the entrance to Jeonju Hanok Village, across from Gyeonggijeon Shrine. Nearby Pungnammun was the south gate of the old Jeonju fortress, and the church site lay on the road just outside it. Where hanok, gate and Western church meet, today's travel route and the martyrdom site of the past overlap within the same city."

"Promoted by Father Baudounet and designed by Father Poisnel, construction began in 1908 and the exterior was completed in 1914. It is Romanesque in style, with rounded arches and a domed bell tower. Stones from the demolished Jeonju fortress wall were used for the foundation, so the old traces of the city hold up the cathedral."
