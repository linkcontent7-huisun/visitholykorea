"A church built where Paul Yun Ji-chung and James Kwon Sang-yeon, Korea's first Catholic martyrs. Pilgrims still encounter that witness here."

"The site stands at 51 Taejo-ro, Wansan-gu, Jeonju-si, Jeollabuk-do (Jeon-dong). Its roads and surrounding landscape help explain why the community formed here."

"It is the starting point of the pilgrimage route through the Hanok Village and Gyeonggijeon to Chimyeongjasan. Their details guide pilgrims through the story."
