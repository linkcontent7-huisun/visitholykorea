"Mission in Gimpo began with the work of Yu Gong-hyeon, catechist of Yakhyeon parish. The Geolpo-ri mission station became an independent parish in 1946 and later took the name Gimpo parish. In 2015 the Diocese of Incheon proclaimed the old stone church the first Eucharistic shrine of the Korean Church."

"The shrine is within Gimpo Church in Bukbyeon-dong, Gimpo. The believers who began the parish at Geolpo-ri secured a new site in the town of Gimpo, but construction was halted by the Korean War. Below the old church on the hill, a new church was built in 1999, so the worship spaces of two eras remain together."

"The old church is a stone building completed in 1956 through the combined efforts of Father Sin In-sik and the faithful, who carried stones between their farm work to take part in the construction. In 2006 the building was restored and fitted out as a retreat space, and a new Way of the Cross was laid out on the hill."
