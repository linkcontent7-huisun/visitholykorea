"During the Byeongin Persecution, James Heo In-baek, Luke Kim Jong-ryun and Peter Yi Yang-deung were brought to the Gyeongju government office. Though interrogated and punished as followers of a forbidden teaching, the three declared themselves Catholics, and were finally sent to Jangdaebeol in Ulsan, where they were martyred."

"This is the site of the office and prison inside the walled town of Joseon-era Gyeongju, where the magistrate tried prisoners. Apartments and a small park in Dongbu-dong now stand where the town wall once was, and the old fortress has vanished, but the memory of the place where the martyrs were held remains."

"The old office and prison buildings disappeared as the city grew, and a small marker stone at the edge of the park now identifies the prison site. Instead of a grand church or memorial, a single stone is all that remains — and that plainness conveys sharply the interrogation and transfer the three blesseds endured."
