"The families of John Yi Yun-il and Joseph Seo Chi-bo hid at Yeoumok in Mungyeong around the time of the Gihae Persecution and founded a village of believers. The catechist Yi Yun-il led nearly thirty believers until his arrest in 1866, and was martyred the following year at Gwandeokjeong in Daegu."

"The Yeoumok village lay on a high pass along the road people from eastern Gyeongsang took to Seoul by way of Mungyeong town and Joryeong. The two families, who had fled from Sangju and Cheongpung, made the mountain path at Jungpyeong-ri in Mungyeong-eup both their hiding place and a route for spreading the faith."

"After the remains of St. Yi Yun-il were moved to the shrine in 1999, a large cross, an outdoor altar, a statue of Mary and a Way of the Cross were raised. Blessed in 2002, Yeoumok Shrine honors together Yi Yun-il, the only saint from Yeongnam among the 103, and the old village of believers."
