"Haemi is where Catholics arrested across the Naepo region were imprisoned and executed. Pius Kim Jin-hu, great-grandfather of Father Kim Taegon, was martyred here too. Beside those whose names survive stand countless victims whose very identities were lost. Haemi Martyrs' Shrine remembers the faith and lives of those nameless people together."

"Haemi Fortress in Seosan, Chungcheongnam-do, held a garrison guarding the west coast. Believers imprisoned inside the fortress were taken out through the west gate and to the banks of the Haemi stream. Yeosutgol, where the shrine now stands, is also ground where martyrdom took place. Fortress and shrine lie apart, but the road between them lets you trace the history of persecution."

"The Memorial Church of the Nameless Martyrs, completed in 2003, was built in a round form, expressing at once the pit of the live burials and eternal life. The stones set into the sanctuary wall stand for the martyrs. In the circular memorial hall beside the church, some of the excavated remains are enshrined, so that the building's shape and its exhibits carry the same memory."
