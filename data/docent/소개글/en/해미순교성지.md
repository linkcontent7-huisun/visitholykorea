"The largest martyrdom site of the Byeongin Persecution of 1866. An estimated 1,000 or more martyrs, including 179 whose names are known."

"The site stands at 13 Seongji 1-ro, Haemi-myeon, Seosan-si, Chungcheongnam-do. Its roads and surrounding landscape help explain why the community formed here."

"Between the church seating seven hundred, the burial ground holding the martyrs' remains, and Jindumbeong where the executions were carried out."
