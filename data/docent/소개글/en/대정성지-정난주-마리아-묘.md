"Mary Jeong Nan-ju, wife of Hwang Sa-yeong, was exiled as a government slave to Daejeong after the silk letter affair of 1801. Parted from her two-year-old son Hwang Gyeong-han on Chuja Island, she kept her faith on Jeju for thirty-seven years and died at sixty-six, honored as a white martyr."

"The grave lies on the broad plain between Sanbangsan and Moseulbong in Daejeong-eup, Seogwipo. The Jeong Nan-ju Trail begins at the grave of the woman villagers called the Seoul grandmother and runs 13.8 kilometers to Moseulpo Church, passing the Alddreu airfield and Seotal Oreum."

"In 1994 the Diocese of Jeju restored the grave and declared it Daejeong Shrine. Inside an arched entrance of Jeju stone walls, a statue of the Virgin and Child in hanbok and the fourteen Stations of the Cross surround a lawn, and beneath a large cross lies the low mound of Mary Jeong Nan-ju's grave."
