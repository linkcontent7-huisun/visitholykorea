"After the Byeongin Persecution, the families of martyrs and their fellow believers gathered in Sinseong-ri, Jeongeup, and formed a community. Martha Hwang, a grandniece of St. Luke Hwang Seok-du, also fled from Yeonpung and settled here, and in 1903, with the appointment of Father Kim Won-yeong, Sinseong parish began."

"The station is in an inland village of Sinseong-ri, Jeongeup, Jeollabuk-do, near the Honam ridge. The name Sinseong did not arise with the arrival of Catholicism but is an old village name found in records of 1789, and together with the surrounding villages of believers around Jeongeup it sheltered refugee Christians."

"The hanok church built in 1909 was the center of the parish in the Jeongeup area until the parish moved to the town in 1936, after which it remained a station. The building, Jeollabuk-do Cultural Heritage Material No. 180, shows early station architecture with the altar and nave set within a hanok frame."
