"Jungnimgul near Daewangam in Ulsan is a coastal cave where believers hid from the authorities during the Byeongin Persecution. Surrounded by sea and rock, the terrain favored concealment, yet it was a hard refuge with limited food and access."

"The faithful gathered in the cave continued to pray together through the persecution, but were finally discovered and arrested. This is not a church or a village of believers but a martyrdom site that conveys the hidden life of people driven to the shore for their faith."

"Today's pilgrimage site lets visitors consider the cave and the memory of the Byeongin Persecution within the coastal scenery of Daewangam. The rock space that remains shows directly the scale in which the believers hid and the danger of their escape route along the sea."
