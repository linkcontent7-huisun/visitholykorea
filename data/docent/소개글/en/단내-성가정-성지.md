"Dannae is a shrine that remembers the faith of Paul Jeong Eun and his grandnephew Peter Jeong Yang-muk. When the ailing Jeong Eun was arrested, Jeong Yang-muk went with him, saying he could not leave his uncle alone, and in 1866 the two were martyred by suffocation at Namhansanseong."

"Dannae in Dancheon-ri, Hobeop-myeon, Icheon, is a village of believers dating from the founding years of the Korean Church. The cave where they hid from persecution and the grave of Jeong Eun remain in the village, which also honors the faith of five of the 103 saints connected with Icheon."

"The shrine church was built on the theme of the Holy Family of Jesus, Mary and Joseph. On the grounds, the grave of Paul Jeong Eun, a martyrs' memorial and a Way of the Cross follow one another, and the path rising from the church to the graves is laid out to trace a faith kept by one family together."
