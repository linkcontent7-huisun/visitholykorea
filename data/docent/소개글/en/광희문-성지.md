"Gwanghuimun was the gate through which the dead were carried out of the capital, and so it was also called Sigumun, the gate of corpses. During the persecutions, the bodies of martyrs left unclaimed at the execution grounds passed through this gate to be discarded or buried in the common graves and hillsides outside the wall."

"Church records count 794 martyrs abandoned or buried outside Gwanghuimun between 1801 and 1879. Of these, 740 were victims from the Byeongin Persecution of 1866 onward, showing the horror of the last years of persecution."

"St. Francis Choe Gyeong-hwan and Blessed Peter Song Baek-dol are among the martyrs connected with Gwanghuimun. The memorial hall near the gate records the names of those brought to Seoul from across the country and the route by which their bodies left the city."
