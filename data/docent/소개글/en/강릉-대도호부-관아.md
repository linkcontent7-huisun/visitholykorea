"The Gangneung Daedohobu government office was the administrative center of Joseon-era Gangneung and the place where believers were interrogated during the Byeongin Persecution. In 1868 Stephen Sim Neung-seok, Anthony Yi Yu-il and others were tortured here before being executed."

"Chilsadang inside the office compound, a tangible cultural heritage of Gangwon, is the building where the local magistrate handled administrative affairs at the time of the persecution. The nearby Imyeonggwan gate, a National Treasure preserving the trace of a Goryeo-era guesthouse, shows the deep layers of the Gangneung office."

"The pilgrim memory here is held not by a separate church but by viewing the office ruins together with the history of martyrdom. The nearby Imdang-dong Church keeps materials on those who preserved the faith in the Gangneung area and a memorial space for the martyrs."
