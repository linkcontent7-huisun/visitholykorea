"Gapgot was a strategic point guarding the Ganghwa Strait, and the ground where Catholics of the Ganghwa area were executed during the Byeongin Persecution. Facing the sea and the old fortification, the site carries the memory of Peter Bak Sun-jip and other forebears who bore witness to the history of persecution."

"The shrine holds a memorial to the martyrs' names and an outdoor altar, and the path opening toward the Ganghwa Strait recalls the execution ground. The grave of Peter Bak Sun-jip is kept here as well, so his work of testimony is remembered together."

"Gapgot Shrine serves as the gateway to the Catholic pilgrimage route of Ganghwa Island and adjoins the history of the nearby Gapgot fortification. Retreats and Masses continue at the shrine's spirituality center, so a martyrdom site beside an old military fort is used as a place of prayer."
