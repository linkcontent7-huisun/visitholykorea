"On 16 August 2014, Pope Francis beatified Paul Yun Ji-chung and 123 companion martyrs in Gwanghwamun Square. It was the first beatification celebrated in person by a pope in Korea, and the names of martyrdom sites from across the country gathered in the heart of Seoul."

"The beatification site is Gwanghwamun Square, where the Gwanghwamun gate of Gyeongbokgung meets Sejong-daero. Before the main gate of the Joseon dynasty and close to the former sites of persecuting bodies such as the Hyeongjo and Uigeumbu, believers who died at the hands of state power were publicly honored."

"The altar and seating were removed after the ceremony, but Gwanghwamun Square remains the beatification site on the Seoul Catholic pilgrimage route. The markers and pilgrimage guides around the square convey the memory of the 124, completed after the beatifications of 79 in 1925 and 24 in 1968."
