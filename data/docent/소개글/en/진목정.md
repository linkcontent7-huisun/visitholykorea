"James Heo In-baek, Luke Kim Jong-ryun and Peter Yi Yang-deung hid in the Beomgul cave at Jinmokjeong to escape the Byeongin Persecution. The three were arrested together in 1868 and martyred at the Ulsan garrison, and were declared blessed during Pope Francis's visit to Korea in 2014."

"The shrine lies halfway up Danseoksan in Sannae-myeon, Gyeongju, and holds the Beomgul cave and the traces of the village where the three blesseds lived. The path climbing from the Sotaegol retreat house to the cave and the path from the Jinmok mission station to the site of the old joint grave form two different pilgrimage routes."

"The martyrs' memorial church and the Heo-Ryun-Yi memorial garden were created by taking one syllable from each of the three blesseds' names. A Way of the Cross follows the mountain path, and the empty grave left after the remains were moved to Gamcheon-ri in 1932 marks the place where they were once buried together."
