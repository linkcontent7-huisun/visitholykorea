"After the Gihae Persecution of 1839, believers hid on Cheonhosan and formed a village of faith. Four saints — Yi Myeong-seo, Son Seon-ji, Jeong Mun-ho and Han Jae-gwon — were martyred in the Byeongin Persecution, and ten martyrs killed at Yeosan were also laid to rest in this mountain graveyard."

"Cheonho Shrine in Bibong-myeon, Wanju, was a hiding place whose deep valleys and mountain paths kept it from the eyes of the authorities. The Mass cave where French missionaries hid to celebrate the Eucharist survives, and the memories of the Cheonhosan village and the Yeosan martyrdom site come together here."

"The grounds hold a graveyard honoring the four saints and the Yeosan martyrs, and a reliquary chapel. The Mass cave on the mountain path, the museum of sacred objects and the retreat spaces lead in turn from the secret worship place of the persecution years to today's pilgrimage church."
