"Thomas Kim Beom-u offered his house at Myeongnyebang as the meeting place of the early community of faith, and was exiled after the Eulsa incident of 1785. On the basis of church-history research, the Diocese of Wonju honors him, the first confessor of the Korean Church, as having died a martyr in Danyang from the after-effects of punishment."

"The shrine was created on the site of the old Danyang Church in Sangbang-ri and Habang-ri, Danseong-myeon, Danyang, Chungcheongbuk-do. In the hall and prison of the Danyang county office just below, not only Kim Beom-u but Yu Sun-ji of the Jeonghae Persecution and Yi Yong-u and Kim Jageunsoe of the Byeongin Persecution were interrogated and imprisoned."

"On 31 May 2026 the Diocese of Wonju celebrated a Mass of proclamation at the old church site and presented the certificate of the Danyang Kim Beom-u Martyrs' Shrine. Pilgrim Masses are offered here, and a history-education program and pilgrimage path linking the county hall and prison are being developed."
