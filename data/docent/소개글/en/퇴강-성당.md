"The faith of Toegang grew as villagers obtained and read books on their own. After Kim Un-bae, Kim Jong-rok and Choe Myeon-jip were baptized in 1899, the faith spread among their neighbors in the clan village. The community that began as the Mulmi mission station in 1903 became a parish in 1922, caring for believers across northern Gyeongsangbuk-do."

"Toegang Church in Sabeolguk-myeon, Sangju, stands near the old Mulmi ferry on the Nakdong River, between the river flowing past the village and the slopes of Joamsan. The land where the church now stands was once the site of the Daegeon Andrea elementary school, and on the hill the site of the earlier church and rectory remains."

"Toegang Church, built of red brick, is a Gothic building with a tall spire and stained-glass windows. On the large rock at the entrance are carved the words cradle of Catholicism in northern Gyeongsangbuk-do. The centenary monument of the Bongdo mission in the yard tells of the people of Toegang who accepted the faith on their own before waiting for missionaries."
