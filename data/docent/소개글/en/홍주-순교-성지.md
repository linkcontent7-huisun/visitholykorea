"Hongju was the administrative and military center of the Naepo region, so believers arrested in the surrounding counties were sent to the Hongju office. From 1791 to the 1870s, 212 martyrs are confirmed by name, and the actual number of victims is estimated at more than a thousand."

"The sites of martyrdom are scattered across Hongju Fortress: the hall where the magistrate interrogated, the prison that held more than a hundred, the execution ground by the Wolgye stream and the site of live burials. In the old market street, believers dragged to the office were paraded in humiliation."

"Peter Won Si-jang, Francis Bang, Laurence Bak Chwi-deuk and Simon Hwang Il-gwang were beatified by Pope Francis in 2014. The pilgrimage route links Anhoedang at the Hongseong county office with the north gate, the prison site and the martyrdom sites along the stream."
