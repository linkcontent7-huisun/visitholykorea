"Sanjeong-dong Church was the first parish of what is now the Archdiocese of Gwangju, founded by Bishop Mutel in 1897, the year Mokpo opened as a port, together with Nabawi Church. It was also the parish where Monsignor Brennan and Fathers Cusack and O'Brien were abducted during the Korean War and lost their lives in Daejeon."

"The shrine sits on Nosong-gil in Sanjeong-dong, looking down on Mokpo harbor, where a parish that once oversaw all of South Jeolla and the first diocesan office stood. In 1953, together with Gyeongdong Church, the first three Legion of Mary praesidia in Korea began their work here."

"The grounds gather the Legion of Mary memorial church and hall, and a history museum converted from the old diocesan office. The red-brick former bishop's residence, built in 1937, survives as a registered national cultural heritage, showing at once the modern architecture of the port city of Mokpo and the beginnings of the diocese."
