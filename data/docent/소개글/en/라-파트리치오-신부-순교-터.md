"Father Patrick Reilly from Ireland came to Korea in 1948, served in Wonju and became pastor of Mukho in 1949. When the Korean War broke out he refused the believers' urging to flee, stayed to guard the church, was arrested, and was martyred on 29 August 1950."

"The official martyrdom site is at San 16-2, Nakpung-ri, Okgye-myeon, Gangneung, near the old Bamjae railway tunnel and the tracks. Being marched with other prisoners from the Mukho security office toward Gangneung, Father Reilly was shot together with his companions on reaching this tunnel in Okgye-myeon."

"Villagers buried the body temporarily nearby, and after UN forces retook Gangneung a military chaplain recovered it. The remains passed through Mukho Church and in 1951 were moved to the clergy graveyard at Jungnim-dong in Chuncheon; he is now among the candidates for beatification with Bishop Hong Yong-ho and 80 companions."
