"Yangyang parish grew from a community of believers who came to the Yeongdong region to escape persecution and was founded in 1921. After the division of the country, Father Timothy Yi Gwang-jae helped clergy and believers from the North to come south. He himself was captured in 1950 and martyred that year at Wonsan."

"Seomun-ri in Yangyang-eup, where the first church stood, was flooded in 1936. The parish secured a new site at Seongnae-ri in the center of town, and Father Yi Gwang-jae completed a church and education hall in 1940. Yangyang was then the corridor through which believers from Yanji, Hamhung and Wonsan traveled south."

"The church destroyed in the war was rebuilt and dedicated in 1954. A memorial space honoring Father Yi Gwang-jae was created on the grounds in 1983, and in 2009 a statue marking the centenary of his birth was raised. Yangyang Church was proclaimed a shrine honoring the life of this martyred priest in 2017."
