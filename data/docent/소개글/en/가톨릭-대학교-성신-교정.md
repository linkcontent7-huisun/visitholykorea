"The Songsin campus carries on the lineage of priestly formation in Korea. After St. Joseph's Seminary at Baeron in Jecheon, founded in 1855, closed in the Byeongin Persecution, the line passed through the Sacred Heart Seminary at Bueonggol in Yeoju in 1885 and the Yongsan seminary in 1887 before reaching Hyehwa-dong."

"The campus stands on Changgyeonggung-ro in Hyehwa-dong, Jongno-gu, Seoul, on the hill behind Daehangno. The Seoul Catholic pilgrimage route links the campus church as a major point between Gwanghuimun gate and the Seokjeong Boreum Well, showing the history of priestly formation in the heart of the city."

"In the crypt of the Songsin campus church rest the remains of St. Andrew Kim Taegon, Korea's first priest. With the seminary church and the formation campus in one place, it directly connects Father Kim's martyrdom with the education of today's seminarians."
