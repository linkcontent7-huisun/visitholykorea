"James Heo In-baek, Luke Kim Jong-ryun and Peter Yi Yang-deung lived with their families in a cave on Danseoksan to escape persecution before they were arrested. Taken through Gyeongju to Ulsan, the three were beheaded together at Jangdaebeol on 14 September 1868."

"Heo In-baek's wife, Bak Jo-ye, buried the three martyrs' bodies temporarily near the execution ground. The remains were moved to Jinmokjeong in 1907 and to the Gamcheon-ri cemetery in Wolbae in 1932, and in 1973 were laid side by side in the sacred precinct of Bokja Church in Sincheon-dong, Daegu."

"The Archdiocese of Daegu built the church in 1970 with donations from the faithful to mark the centenary of the Byeongin Persecution. Its form evokes the Raphael, the boat on which Father Kim Taegon returned from China, and the sacred precinct before the main entrance holds the remains of the three blesseds."
