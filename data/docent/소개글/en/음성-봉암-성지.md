"Bongam carries on the history of the Bangchukgol and Gyemadae villages, where believers lived together after the Gihae Persecution. Father Choe Yang-eop visited to administer the sacraments and chose John the Apostle Kim, youngest son of Kim Baek-sim, as a student of the Baeti seminary. In the Byeongin Persecution, Kim Baek-sim, Min Yun-myeong and others were arrested and martyred in Seoul and Chungju."

"The shrine is in Bonghyeon-ri, Maengdong-myeon, Eumseong, in a farming village near the Chungbuk Innovation City. The name Bangchukgol came from the Bongam reservoir, near which the Gyemadae village stood. The community scattered by persecution gathered again when Jeong Mu-seon moved to Bongam village, founding a mission station in 1897."

"The station building and the cross at the village entrance are the center of Bongam Shrine. This is not an execution ground or a grave but a place carrying on the faith of the village where the martyrs lived. The Diocese of Cheongju proclaimed the shrine in 2020, and parishioners of Maengdong took part in a land-donation campaign to create the pilgrimage space."
