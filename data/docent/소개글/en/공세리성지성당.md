"Gongseri was both a base for spreading the faith through the Naepo region and a shrine that remembers its martyrs. The three Bak brothers — Ui-seo, Won-seo and Ik-seo — who were taken to Suwon and killed during the persecutions, came from Geolmae-ri here. The memorial space beside the church honors the lives of thirty-two martyrs from the Gongseri area of Asan."

"On this hill near Asan Bay stood Gongse-chang, the state granary that gathered grain owed in tax. After the warehouse that collected tax rice from many counties was gone, a church and rectory rose on its site. The place name Gongseri and the ancient trees remain, letting visitors meet the village's history from before the faith arrived."

"The present red-brick church was designed by Father Devise, built with Chinese craftsmen, and completed in 1922. Its pointed bell tower and windows appear among the old trees. The former rectory is now a museum, telling the story of the martyrs, the building of the church, and the Yi Myeong-rae ointment."
