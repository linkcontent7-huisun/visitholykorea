"Waegogae in Yongsan took its name from Waseo, the government office that fired roof tiles and bricks, and was called Wahyeon or Waseohyeon. Today it holds the offices of the Military Ordinariate and the Military Cathedral."

"The remains of Bishop Imbert and Fathers Maubant and Chastan, martyred at Saenamteo in 1839, were temporarily buried here. The remains of Father Kim Taegon, martyred in 1846, also rested briefly at Waegogae before being moved to Mirinae."

"Seven martyrs of the Byeongin Persecution lay in this ground for thirty-three years, and two Seosomun martyrs for forty-three. In 2013 the Military Ordinariate created a memorial, a large cross and a Way of the Cross to mark the burial place used before their remains were transferred."
