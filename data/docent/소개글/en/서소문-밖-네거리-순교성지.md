"The crossroads outside Seosomun Gate was Joseon's official execution ground, where countless believers were martyred from the Sinyu Persecution of 1801 to the Byeongin Persecution of 1866. Forty-four of the 103 Korean Martyr Saints and twenty-seven of the 124 blesseds gave their lives here."

"In 2014, before the beatification Mass at Gwanghwamun, Pope Francis visited the Seosomun martyrs' memorial tower to pray. It is also the central shrine of the Way of Life, one of the Seoul pilgrimage routes approved by the Holy See in 2018."

"The Seosomun Shrine History Museum, opened in 2019, links an underground exhibition hall with a sky plaza, unfolding the memory of martyrdom as space. The remains of the late-Joseon execution ground and the martyrs' records can be studied together beneath the city center."
