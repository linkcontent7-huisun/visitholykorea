"In Naju, Peter Yi Chun-hwa was martyred in 1839, and Paul Kang Yeong-won, Andrew Yu Chi-seong and Paul Yu Mun-bo in 1872 at Muhakdang. In honor of the four martyrs of Muhakdang, a military training ground and execution site, Naju parish became a martyrs' memorial church."

"The church stands in Sanjeong-dong, Naju, close to the old Muhakdang. In 2001, ten of the twelve foundation stones remaining at the martyrdom site on the grounds of Naju Elementary School were moved here to form a monument, gathering within the parish the only traces of a martyrdom site in the Archdiocese of Gwangju."

"The church built in 1935, when the mission was raised to a parish, was the first church the Columban Fathers built in Korea. In 2004 a martyrs' memorial chapel in the form of an empty tomb was added, and the Western-style brick rectory of 1934 and the hanok used by the Caritas Sisters were restored."
