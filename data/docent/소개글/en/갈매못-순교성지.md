"At Galmaemot on 30 March 1866, Bishop Daveluy, Fathers Aumaître and Huin, Luke Hwang Seok-du and Joseph Jang Ju-gi were martyred. It was Good Friday, and all five were declared saints in 1984."

"Galmaemot in Ocheon-myeon, Boryeong, is a sandy beach on the Yellow Sea once watched over by the Chungcheong naval command. With a royal wedding approaching, the execution ground was moved here to avoid bloodshed near the capital; the remains of four of the saints later passed through Seojitgol and Nagasaki before reaching Jeoldusan."

"The Church of Our Lady of Victory above the martyrdom site has a roof shaped like a seashell and an altar facing the sea. When the sliding stained-glass panels behind the altar are opened, the Yellow Sea becomes the sanctuary's backdrop, and a memorial to the blessed martyrs raised in 1975 marks the old execution ground."
