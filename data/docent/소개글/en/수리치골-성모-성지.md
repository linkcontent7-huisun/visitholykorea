"Surichigol, in a deep valley of Guksabong in Gongju, is a village of believers formed to escape persecution. On 2 November 1846, Bishop Ferréol and Father Daveluy founded here the Confraternity of the Immaculate Heart of Mary, Korea's first official Marian devotional association."

"The missionaries hiding from the Byeongo Persecution prayed that the Church of Joseon would overcome its hardships under the protection of Mary. Once the dwelling of a single isolated believing family, Surichigol became the center of faith for the surrounding villages after the confraternity was founded."

"A sculpture of the Holy Trinity was raised in 1990, and the path of the Seven Sorrows of Mary was laid out in 1993. In 1997 the motherhouse of the Mirinae Sisters of the Immaculate Heart of Mary, which carries on the confraternity's purpose, moved to this shrine."
