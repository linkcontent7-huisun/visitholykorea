"At Cheonjinam, Yi Byeok and young scholars gathered for the lectures led by Kwon Cheol-sin and studied Western learning and Catholic doctrine. At Yi Byeok's urging, Yi Seung-hun was baptized in Beijing and returned in 1784, founding a Korean Catholic community that began without missionaries."

"The shrine lies on the site of an old hermitage on the slopes of Aengjabong in Toechon-myeon, Gwangju, Gyeonggi-do. It was the place of mountain lectures where scholars debated the scriptures by candlelight, and beyond the fork at the entrance lie the graves of Saints Jeong Ha-sang and Yu Jin-gil, who worked to bring in clergy and establish an independent vicariate for Joseon."

"After the value of the lecture site was rediscovered in the 1960s, land was acquired from 1980 and a Carmelite convent and a bicentenary monument were built. The graveyard holds the five founding ancestors: Yi Byeok, Kwon Cheol-sin, Kwon Il-sin, Yi Seung-hun and Jeong Yak-jong."
