"Victorinus Bak Dae-sik, from Jillye in Gimhae, was arrested in 1868 together with his nephew Bak Su-yeon. He did not renounce his faith even under bone-breaking torture at the Gimhae office and the Daegu gamyeong, and that year he was martyred at Gwandeokjeong with Matthew Song and Joseph Bak."

"The family wished to bury the martyr in their ancestral hill, but the clan objected, so he was buried without a mound on land of the Yu clan in Cheongcheon-ri, Jillye-myeon. The remains have never been moved; in 1956 a mound was raised and his wife's grave was moved beside it."

"In 1966 Father Yu Chang-ho of Jinyeong parish set a headstone before the grave, though his baptismal name was not yet known with certainty. Through church-history research and the efforts of his descendants, the name Victorinus was confirmed in 2001, and after the beatification of 2014 the grave became a shrine of the blessed."
