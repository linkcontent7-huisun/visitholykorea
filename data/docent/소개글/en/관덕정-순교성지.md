"Gwandeokjeong was a Joseon-era military examination ground and execution site, where twenty-five Catholics of the Yeongnam region gave their lives between the Eulhae Persecution of 1815 and 1868. John Yi Yun-il, arrested while serving as a catechist in Mungyeong and Sangju, was martyred here by military beheading in 1867, and in 1984 became the only saint from Yeongnam among the 103 Korean Martyrs."

"The shrine is a fifteen-minute walk from Daegu Station, among the office buildings of Namsan-dong. This is the execution ground to which believers were brought after interrogation in the prison of Gyeongsang Gamyeong and confirmation of their sentence. The gamyeong prison site and Gwandeokjeong are two scenes of martyrdom that can be joined on foot within the city center."

"A tiled-roof pavilion rising among grey buildings is the first impression of this shrine. In the underground church, the altar holds the remains of St. Yi Yun-il, moved here from Mirinae in 1986, and beneath the painted eaves of the rooftop pavilion are carved the Alpha and Omega, so that traditional Korean architecture and the symbols of faith meet within one building."
