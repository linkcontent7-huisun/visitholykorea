"The Seongnae-dong community in Samcheok began in 1946 as a mission station of Gangneung parish and became a parish in 1949. Its first pastor, Father McGinn of the Missionary Society of St. Columban, was killed by North Korean troops on 4 July 1950 and died a martyr."

"His successor, Father Connors, led the building of the church after the war; construction began in March 1957 and the church was consecrated that November. The stone building of about 440 square meters was then the largest church in the Diocese of Chuncheon."

"Seongnae-dong Church became National Registered Cultural Heritage No. 141 in 2004 for showing the regional stone-building techniques of the 1950s. Though its jurisdiction changed when the Diocese of Wonju was created in 1965, it continues to honor the memory of Father McGinn's martyrdom."
