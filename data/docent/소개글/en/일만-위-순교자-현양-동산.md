"The Hill of the Ten Thousand Martyrs was created to honor the martyrs of Korea whose names have not been handed down. Begun by the Catholic University of Incheon in 1996, it was dedicated by the Diocese of Incheon in 2002. It is not the grave of any one martyr but a memorial space for the nameless."

"The hill lies on the grounds of the Star of the Sea youth center in Gocheon-ri, Naega-myeon, Ganghwa. A valley separates the center's facilities from the pilgrimage space. On this hill on Ganghwa Island run a martyrs' Way of the Cross and a path for meditating on the sorrows of Mary."

"A chapel making use of the Mumyeonggwan hall and a martyrs' memorial church stand on the hill. The St. Nam Jong-sam Memorial Hall, dedicated in 2007, enshrines part of the remains of John Nam Jong-sam, who died in the Byeongin Persecution. The statue of the unknown martyr and the saint's relics convey together the memory of those without names and those whose names survive."
