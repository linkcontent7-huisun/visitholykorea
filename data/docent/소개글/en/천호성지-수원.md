"In the Toechon area of Gwangju, believers continued after the Cheonjinam lectures, and records tell that the family of Peter Son Seong-jik, martyred in the Byeongin Persecution, moved from Somoe in this area. Cheonho Shrine is a historic site of the Diocese of Suwon preserving the memory of the Toechon village of believers and its martyrs."

"The shrine is in Toechon-myeon, Gwangju, Gyeonggi-do. It differs in both diocese and region from the widely known Cheonho Shrine of the Diocese of Jeonju in Wanju, Jeollabuk-do; this one remembers the mountain community of faith of Toechon in Gwangju."

"The heart of this place is the site of the Toechon village and its martyrdom history rather than a large church building. It should be viewed within the history of Toechon parish of the Diocese of Suwon and the flow of faith in the Gwangju area leading to Cheonjinam, without confusing it with the Cheonho Shrine of Jeonju."
