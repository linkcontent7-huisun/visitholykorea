"At Eunyi in 1836, fifteen-year-old Andrew Kim Taegon received baptism and First Communion from Father Maubant and was chosen as a seminarian. Returning as a priest, he made this his first parish, and on Easter Sunday 1846 he celebrated his last public Mass here."

"Eunyi in Cheoin-gu, Yongin, is a village of believers ringed by mountains, as its name — hidden village — suggests. It connects to Golbaemasil, where Kim Taegon grew up, and is also the road where, after his martyrdom, his body paused to bid farewell to his mother Ursula Go on the way to Mirinae."

"The Kim Taegon Memorial Church at the shrine revives the appearance and scale of the Jinjiaxiang church in Shanghai where he was ordained. In the yard stands the Kim Taegon memorial pavilion, a reconstruction of the 1947 memorial chapel of Namgok-ri Church, and the Young Kim Taegon Trail continues on to Mirinae."
