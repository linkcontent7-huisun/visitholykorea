"After the Byeongin Persecution, believers who fled from Chungcheong into the mountains of Jeolla formed the Eoeun-dong village. In 1900 Father Stephen Kim Yang-hong was appointed and opened the parish of the Jinan area; of its eleven stations and 999 believers at the time, 189 lived in Eoeun-dong."

"The station is in Eoeun-dong, Juksan-ri, Jinan-eup, Jinan, Jeollabuk-do. At nearby Mosigol the remains of St. Yi Myeong-seo, moved from Yusang-ri in Soyang, lay buried until their transfer to Jeoldusan, and Eoeun-dong became the center of ministry in the mountains of Jinan."

"Father Kim Yang-hong built a hanok church in 1904 and then enlarged it, completing in 1909 a wooden church roofed with stone shingles. The believers themselves carried the slate from Baegun-myeon and the floor timbers, and the building, preserving an interior divided between men's and women's seats, is National Registered Cultural Heritage No. 28."
