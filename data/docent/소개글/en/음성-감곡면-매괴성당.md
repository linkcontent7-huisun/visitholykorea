"Gamgok parish, founded in 1896 by the French missionary Father Camille Bouillon, was the first parish in Chungcheongbuk-do. He served here for fifty-one years, founding a school where children learned the Korean alphabet, and after his death he was buried in the grave beside the church."

"The church stands on a hillside in Woljeong-ri, Gamgok-myeon, Eumseong, looking down over the Janghowon area. Father Bouillon chose as the parish site the grounds of Min Eung-sik's house, which he had seen on a pastoral visit, and the church on the hill and the village below became the base of mission in northern Chungbuk."

"The present church, completed in 1930, is a red-brick Gothic building marked by a tall central bell tower and pointed-arch windows. Holding inside the statue of Our Lady of the Rosary and, beside it, the grave of Father Bouillon, it shows the character of both a modern church building and a Marian pilgrimage site."
