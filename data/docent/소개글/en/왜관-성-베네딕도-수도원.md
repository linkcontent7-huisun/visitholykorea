"The roots of Waegwan Abbey reach back to the German Benedictine missionaries who came to Korea in 1909 and to the monasteries at Wonsan and Deokwon. Monks who fled south during the Korean War refounded the community at Waegwan in Chilgok, Gyeongsangbuk-do, in 1952."

"The abbey honors Saints Maurus and Placid as its patrons and follows the Benedictine tradition of pray and work. It continues liturgy, farming and crafts, and publishes books of theology and spirituality through Benedict Press."

"In 1964 Waegwan was raised to an abbey, and Father Odo Haas was elected its first abbot. It carries on the tradition of the Deokwon community while standing as the central monastery of the Benedictines in Korea."
