"Joseph Cho Yun-ho was arrested with his father Cho Hwa-seo in the Byeongin Persecution of 1866. After his father was martyred first at Supjeongi in Jeonju, the son lost his life at Seocheon Bridge. Cho Yun-ho, then eighteen, and his father Cho Hwa-seo were declared saints together in 1984."

"The martyrdom site is on the bank of the Jeonju stream near Seocheon Bridge in Seowansan-dong, Jeonju. Father and son were interrogated at the Jeolla Gamyeong, but their final execution grounds differed. The father's Supjeongi and the son's Seocheon Bridge each convey the last road of a father and son separated in the Byeongin Persecution."

"In 2006 the Diocese of Jeonju installed a mosaic mural near Seocheon Bridge depicting the martyrdom of St. Cho Yun-ho. The mural on the riverside bicycle path and a marker stone identify the site. Instead of a large church, the memorials beside the bridge and the stream keep this young man's name alive."
