"The Yongsomak community in Sillim-myeon, Wonju, began in 1898 as a mission station in the thatched house of the believer Choe Seok-du. In 1904 it was established as the third parish in Gangwon-do, after Pungsuwon and Wonju."

"The first pastor, Father Poyaud, used the converted thatched house as a church until the present red-brick church was completed in 1915. Its pointed bell tower, arched windows and long single nave show Western church architecture in a rural parish."

"Yongsomak Church was designated a tangible cultural heritage of Gangwon in 1986. It preserves together the community of faith that continued from the mission-station years before the parish was founded, and the brick architecture of the Wonju area in the early twentieth century."
