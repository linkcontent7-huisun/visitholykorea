"John the Baptist Yi Byeok, known as Gwangam, was born in Hwahyeon-ri, Pocheon, in 1754 and shared his studies with the brothers Jeong Yak-jeon and Jeong Yak-yong. At the Cheonjinam lectures of 1779 he embraced the Catholic faith deeply, and he served as the first leader of the Korean Church founded by laypeople."

"Hwahyeon-ri in Pocheon holds together the site of Yi Byeok's birthplace and the site of his original grave. His remains were moved to Cheonjinam in 1979, but the city of Pocheon designated the old grave Local Heritage No. 48, and the Diocese of Chuncheon proclaimed the two sites a shrine in 2023."

"The Gwangam Yi Byeok Memorial Church is designed so that only the sky can be seen from a courtyard enclosed by walls, expressing a life that looked to God even under persecution within his own family. Beside it, a reconstruction hall of the birthplace, a memorial hall and a Way of the Cross convey his two faces as scholar of practical learning and leader of faith."
