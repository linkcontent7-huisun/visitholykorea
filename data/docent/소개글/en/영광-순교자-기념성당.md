"In the early years of the Joseon Church, a community of faith took root in Yeonggwang through the preaching of Yu Hang-geom. But when the Sinyu Persecution struck in 1801, Yi Hwa-baek and Mr. O of Boksan-ri were executed here, and those who had shared their faith were martyred in Jeonju or exiled to Hwanghae Province. The church remembers the traces of that first faith."

"The church is in the town of Yeonggwang, Jeollanam-do. Past the martyrs' memorial gate, a statue of the martyrs stands in a modest yard, and in this quiet town church you meet the first faith that took root in this land two centuries ago. The site bears witness that believers lived even in a small county on the Honam coast before the persecutions."

"The parish church was built in 1937, closed, and reopened in 1965. When the Archdiocese of Gwangju designated it a martyrs' memorial church in 2010, the memorial gate, the statue of the martyrs and a memorial exhibition hall were raised in turn. Rather than a grand building, the monuments in its yard quietly convey the history of martyrdom."
