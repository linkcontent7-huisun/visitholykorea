"This park in Yongdae-ri, Gunwi, was created around the site of the birthplace where Cardinal Stephen Kim Sou-hwan was born and spent his childhood. The project began in 2012 and the park opened in 2018, with the birthplace and the earthenware kiln restored."

"The memorial hall, opened with the park in 2018, builds its exhibition around the family's trade of making earthenware, and its ceiling reflects the form of a cross. His pastoral work and his message of love and sharing can be traced through personal belongings and documents."

"Beyond the birthplace and memorial hall, the park continues with a chapel, a Way of the Cross and a memorial garden. It is a pilgrimage space that connects, in one place, the boyhood spent in Gunwi and the life he left as the first cardinal of the Korean Church."
