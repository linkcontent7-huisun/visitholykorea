"The Uigeumbu was the office directly under the king that interrogated serious offenders. Foreign clergy who worked in Joseon — Bishop Imbert and Fathers Maubant and Chastan in 1839, Bishop Berneux and Father de Bretenières in 1866 — were interrogated here and sentenced to death."

"The martyrdom site is in Jongno 1-ga, Jongno-gu, Seoul, in front of exit 1 of Jonggak Station. Set within the judicial center of the persecution years, where the Left and Right Police Bureaus, the Jeonokseo prison and the Hyeongjo gathered, and included in the Seoul pilgrimage route approved by the Holy See in 2018, it shows the process by which foreign missionaries, once arrested, received the king's judgment."

"In place of the old Uigeumbu building, a marker stone and the Seoul Catholic pilgrimage route identify the spot. The distinctive character of this urban martyrdom site is that one finds the trial ground of Bishop Berneux and the three French missionaries between the commercial buildings and the subway station of Jongno."
