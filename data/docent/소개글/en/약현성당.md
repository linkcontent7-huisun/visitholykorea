"Yakhyeon Church began in 1891 as the second parish of Seoul. It is a church that remembers those who lost their lives outside Seosomun Gate for keeping their faith. On the parish's centenary, a memorial hall for the Seosomun martyrs was built on its grounds. Here the worship of gathered believers and the honoring of the martyrs continue side by side."

"The church stands on a hill in Jungnim-dong, Seoul. The name Yakhyeon comes from the herb fields that once lay nearby. Below the hill is the crossroads outside Seosomun, an execution ground in the Joseon era. This setting, looking down on the site of martyrdom, is a key to understanding the church's history together with the city around it."

"Designed and supervised by Father Coste and built in 1892, it is the oldest surviving Western-style church building in Korea. Red brick and a modest bell tower draw the eye. Inside, the central space is finished with pointed arches and the side aisles with rounded ones, so that different curves meet within a small church."
