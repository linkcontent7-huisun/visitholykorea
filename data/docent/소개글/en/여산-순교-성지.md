"In the Mujin Persecution of 1868, believers arrested in Gosan, Geumsan and Jinsan were brought to the Yeosan county office. Martyrs including the twenty-two confirmed by records lost their lives at the county hall, the prison and Supjeongi by beheading, hanging and suffocation with wet paper, making this a martyrdom site of the Diocese of Jeonju."

"As the gateway of Honam linking Chungcheong and Jeolla, Yeosan had the office to which believers arrested in the mountain villages were gathered. Even now the county hall, the prison site, Supjeongi and the paper-execution site are scattered within Yeosan-myeon, so the pilgrim walks not one place but seven martyrdom sites."

"At Supjeongi stand a large memorial engraved with the martyrs' names and an outdoor altar, and across the road the Gothic Yeosan Church guards the hill. The statue of Our Lady, Mother of Martyrs, patroness of the shrine, was made to show Mary beneath the cross gazing upon the martyrs of Yeosan."
