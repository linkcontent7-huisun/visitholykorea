"At the Ulsan garrison, O Chi-mun was martyred in 1860, and in 1868 James Heo In-baek, Luke Kim Jong-ryun and Peter Yi Yang-deung were executed. The three were transported from Gyeongju and lost their lives here. The remains recovered by Heo In-baek's wife Bak Jo-ye were moved several times and are now enshrined at Bokja Church in Daegu."

"This martyrdom site in Namoe-dong, Jung-gu, Ulsan, lies on the bank of the Dongcheon stream. At the garrison of the Joseon-era military commander of Gyeongsang Left Province, soldiers were drilled and serious offenders executed. The stone platform from which the commander gave orders to the troops was called the jangdae, and the name Jangdaebeol, the field before it, remains at the martyrdom site."

"From 1979 the site was developed with a martyrs' memorial, a statue of Father Kim Taegon and an outdoor stone altar. In 2014 a two-story church was dedicated. That same year Heo In-baek, Kim Jong-ryun and Yi Yang-deung were declared blessed, and the riverside monuments and the church became a space remembering the three martyrs together."
