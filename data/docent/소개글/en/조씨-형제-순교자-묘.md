"The brothers Cho Seok-bin and Cho Seok-jeung were arrested in 1868 while teaching the catechism after entering the Church. Refusing the demand at the Dongnae office to abandon their faith, they were martyred at Waejangdae in Gimhae. When the elder brother died first, the younger also rejected all persuasion and chose the same path."

"Denied burial in their family's ancestral hill, the two brothers were laid on the hill behind the house of Bae Jeong-mun in Saenggok-dong, Busan. The Bae family guarded the graves for generations. For pilgrimage and preservation, the Diocese of Busan moved the remains in September 2026 to the Paul Jeong Ha-sang Spirituality Center in Yangsan."

"The new grave in Yangsan is a flat grave laid close to the ground without a mound. It reflects historical research on early burial customs, and on 15 September 2026 Bishop Son Sam-seok presided over the burial rite. The pilgrimage space honoring the brothers continues at the new graveyard."
