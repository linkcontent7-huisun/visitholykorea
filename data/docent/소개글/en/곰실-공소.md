"The layman Martin Eom Ju-eon, baptized at Cheonjinam, returned to Gomsil and preached the gospel to his neighbors. The community grew until forty to fifty people were baptized each time Father Jeong Gyu-ha visited, and in 1920, when Father Kim Yu-ryong was appointed, it became the first parish of Chuncheon."

"The mission station is in the old village of Gomsil in Goeun-ri, Dongnae-myeon, Chuncheon. Bishop Mutel's diary of 1900 records one large house at the edge of the plain, three families and ten people who made their confession, conveying the beginnings of the Chuncheon community of faith."

"The station building of 1920 burned in the Korean War and was rebuilt after the armistice. The Diocese of Chuncheon renovated the building in 2008 and in 2009 set a marker naming it the cradle of the diocese, designating it a historic site; today Geodu-ri Church oversees the station."
