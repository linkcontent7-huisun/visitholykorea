"The Church of Repentance and Atonement at Tongil-dongsan in Paju was built to repent of the fratricidal Korean War and to pray for reconciliation between North and South. A group of believers from Sariwon in North Korea secured the site in 1997, and construction began in 2006."

"The exterior of the church is a hanok modeled on Jinsa-dong Church in Sinuiju, built by the Maryknoll Society in 1923. The interior recreates the prewar St. Benedict Abbey at Deokwon, holding the memory of the Church in the North."

"The church was consecrated in 2013, the sixtieth anniversary of the armistice, and beside it stands the National Reconciliation Center opened in 2014. Through reconciliation Masses and prayer meetings in the border area, it reflects on both the wounds of division and the task of peace."
