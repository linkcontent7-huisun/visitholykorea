"Felix Peter Kim Gi-ryang, a seaman from Hamdeok, was rescued while adrift in 1857, learned the catechism in Hong Kong and was baptized. The first person from Jeju to be baptized, he spread the faith in his hometown until he was arrested at Geoje during the Byeongin Persecution and martyred at Tongyeong in 1867."

"The memorial hall stands in the seaside village of Hamdeok-ri, Jocheon-eup, Jeju, where Kim Gi-ryang was born and went to sea. His livelihood sailing between Jeju and the mainland led to his drifting to Hong Kong, his baptism and his return to evangelize, becoming the channel through which the Church in Jeju began."

"In 2020 the Diocese of Jeju created a memorial monument, hall, chapel and pilgrimage path for Kim Gi-ryang at Hamdeok-ri. The exhibition follows his drifting, his baptism in Hong Kong and his martyrdom at Tongyeong, presenting the life of Jeju's first martyr, beatified in 2014."
