"Gyeonggi Gamyeong was the office administering Gyeonggi Province in the Joseon era, and the place where arrested Catholics were interrogated and punished. Peter Cho Yong-sam, martyred here, was declared blessed during Pope Francis's visit to Korea in 2014."

"The site lies around the former Red Cross Hospital in Migeun-dong, Seodaemun-gu, Seoul. On the road from Seodaemun to the crossroads outside Seosomun, it lets one read together the trial and execution route of martyrs who passed the sites of the Hyeongjo and the Right Police Bureau."

"No buildings of the office survive; the site is remembered as urban ground now occupied by the hospital and roads. The Way of Life on the Seoul Catholic pilgrimage route connects the Gyeonggi Gamyeong site with the Seosomun martyrs' shrine, revealing the spatial structure of persecution that led from office to execution ground."
