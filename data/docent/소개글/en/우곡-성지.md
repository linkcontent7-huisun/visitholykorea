"Ugok Shrine honors the faith of Hong Yu-han, called Korea's first ascetic, and of his descendants. Around 1750 Hong Yu-han encountered books of Western learning and began a life of self-denial without baptism, and thirteen of his descendants were martyred in the persecutions."

"Ugok-ri in Bongseong-myeon, Bonghwa, is a mountain valley close to Gugu-ri in Yeongju, where Hong Yu-han spent his last years. As his descendants settled and were buried here, the old house site became the place of his ascetic life and Ugok a resting place holding the family's history of martyrdom."

"The graves and memorial space of Ugok Shrine present Hong Yu-han's thirteen descendants as one lineage. Among them, the names of the brothers Peter Hong Byeong-ju and Paul Hong Yeong-ju, two of the 103 saints, and of several blesseds are engraved in the family graveyard in the mountains of Bonghwa."
