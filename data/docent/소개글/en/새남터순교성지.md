"Saenamteo on the bank of the Han River was a Joseon military training ground and the execution site for crimes against the state. From 1801 to 1866, eleven clergy were martyred here, including St. Andrew Kim Taegon, Korea's first native priest."

"At Saenamteo, Bishop Imbert and Fathers Maubant and Chastan were martyred in 1839, and Bishop Berneux and his fellow missionaries in 1866. Unlike Seosomun, where many lay martyrs died, this is why it is known as the martyrdom site of the clergy."

"The present memorial church was built in 1967, with a roof modeled on traditional architecture and a memorial hall. The church holds a space honoring the remains and relics of the Saenamteo martyrs, including Father Kim Taegon."
