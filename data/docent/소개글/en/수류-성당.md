"Suryu parish began in 1889 in the mountain village of Baejae. When Father Lacrouts bought the ancestral hall of the scholar Yi Yeong-sam at Suryu in 1895 and converted it into a church and rectory, the parish moved as well. Believers from various places who had found it hard to attend Mass moved in, forming a village where the residents lived the faith together."

"The church stands on a broad hill at the entrance to Sanghwa village in Hwayul-ri, Geumsan-myeon, Gimje, facing Sangdusan. It is the site to which the parish came out from Baejae in the valley of Moaksan for easier access. Opposite the church stands a great zelkova tree, and the village and church appeared in the film Summer of Boriul, a story of children's football."

"The wooden church completed in 1907 burned during the Korean War. The believers saved relief supplies to fund the building and brought sand and gravel from the stream to make bricks. In this way they built the present church in 1959, reusing for the first roof the sheet metal salvaged from the burned old church."
