"This pilgrimage site on the slopes of Hongboksan in Yangju holds the grave of Alexius Hwang Sa-yeong, martyred in the Sinyu Persecution of 1801. He was arrested while writing, in the earthen cave at Baeron in Jecheon, his silk letter describing the persecution and a plan to rebuild the Church."

"Hwang Sa-yeong was taken to Seoul and executed as a traitor, and his body was not returned to his family. His grave was identified in 1980 on the basis of tradition passed down among his descendants, and was developed as a pilgrimage site."

"A stone cross stands in the graveyard, and a marker telling that the body was wrapped in silk and preserved in a jar conveys the secret burial. The silk-letter cave at Baeron, where he wrote in 1801, and the grave at Yangju link his final footsteps across two different places."
