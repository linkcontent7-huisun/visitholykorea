"The history of faith in Pocheon continues through the lives of Hong Gyo-man and his son Hong In. The father was martyred outside Seosomun in Seoul during the Sinyu Persecution, and the son Hong In was taken to Pocheon and lost his life in 1802. The two were declared blessed together in 2014."

"The martyrdom site of Leo Hong In lies on the sandbank of the Hannae stream near the Pocheon Bridge in Gueup-ri, Gunnae-myeon, Pocheon. Seoul, where the father died, and Pocheon, where the son died, are different places. The Diocese of Chuncheon proclaimed this Pocheon martyrdom site a shrine in 2014, after the beatification of father and son."

"The old Pocheon Church connected with the shrine is a stone building erected in 1955 with the help of General Yi Han-rim and the army. Granite walls, a bell tower and pointed-arch windows are its features. Even after a fire in 1990 the old church has been preserved, so that church architecture of the immediate postwar years can be studied here."
