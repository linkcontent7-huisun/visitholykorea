"The nineteenth letter of Father Choe Yang-eop tells of a devout old woman who founded a village of believers at Nalmoe in Daegu. Here the family of Yi Eung-chil buried and protected the body of St. John Yi Yun-il, martyred at Gwandeokjeong during the Byeongin Persecution, on the hill behind Nalmoe."

"The church site was one of the execution grounds used by the Gyeongsang gamyeong, and Father Robert's report of 1887 recorded thirty believers at Nalmoe. As the community grew after the diocese was established, it was raised in 1927 to become Daegu's third parish."

"In 1931 the Myeongdohoe hall building of Namsan parish was moved here to form the frame of the new church. After several phases of construction, the present church was consecrated on 29 January 1958, and, still holding the structure of the old hall, it continues the path by which St. Yi Yun-il's remains were carried."
