"Jungnim-dong Church began in 1920 when the Gomsil mission station was raised to a parish. It holds the graves of the priests and religious killed in Chuncheon during the Korean War, and in 2017 the Diocese of Chuncheon declared this graveyard a martyrs' shrine."

"The church stands in Jungnim-dong below Yaksa Pass in Chuncheon, carrying the faith of the Gomsil village of believers into the city. After the Chuncheon prefecture was separated from Seoul in 1939 it became the diocesan center, and the cathedral and the wartime martyrs' graveyard share one precinct."

"Construction of the church began in 1949 under Bishop Quinlan but was halted by the war, and the building was damaged. It was completed in 1953 with help from the U.S. Army and the Holy See; renovation began in 1998, and in 2013 the graveyard was consecrated as a shrine."
