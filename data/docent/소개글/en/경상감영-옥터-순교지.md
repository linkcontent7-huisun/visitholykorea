"During the persecutions, Catholics arrested across Gyeongsang Province were held and interrogated in the prison of Gyeongsang Gamyeong. Seven of them, including Francis Choe Bong-han, Alexius Kim Si-u, Paul Bak Gyeong-hwa and Richard An Gun-sim, died in this prison. Those whose sentences were confirmed were taken to the nearby Gwandeokjeong execution ground, so the gamyeong and Gwandeokjeong are two scenes of one story."

"The site is in the heart of downtown Daegu, around today's Gyeongsang Gamyeong Park and Daean Church. It was the seat of the governor who ruled Gyeongsang Province in the Joseon era, and beneath a park where citizens now rest there once stood a harsh prison. Within walking distance of Daegu Station, a stroll through the city center passes the place of martyrdom."

"The old prison buildings no longer survive, and Daean Church beside the park has become the church that keeps this memory. The gamyeong's main gate and the governor's office building have been restored inside the park, so the form of the government compound and the history of the interrogations that took place within it can be recalled together."
