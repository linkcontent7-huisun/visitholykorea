"Solmoe is the birthplace of Korea's first native priest, St. Andrew Kim Taegon, born here in 1821. It was the home of a family that gave martyrs across four generations, from his great-grandfather Kim Jin-hu to Father Kim himself. Leaving home with his family as a boy, he studied in Macau, became a priest, and was martyred at Saenamteo in 1846."

"Solmoe, the name of a small village in Ugang-myeon, Dangjin, Chungcheongnam-do, means a little hill thick with pines. Father Kim's family, who lived here, moved to Golbaemasil in Yongin to escape persecution. Today it is a pilgrimage site where visitors walk the restored birthplace and the pine grove and meet the faith of the Naepo region carried on within one family."

"On the site of the birthplace, a hanok in the style of a Naepo gentry house was restored in 2004. The shrine's arts complex, Memory and Hope, was built to mark the two-hundredth anniversary of Father Kim's birth. Beneath a roof shaped like wild-rose petals, a church and exhibition spaces gather, so that an old house and modern architecture together tell the saint's life."
