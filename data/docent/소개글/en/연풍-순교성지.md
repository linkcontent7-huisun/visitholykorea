"Yeonpung, at the approach to Mungyeong Saejae pass, is where believers traveling between Chungcheong and Gyeongsang were arrested and interrogated at the county office. During the Byeongin Persecution of 1866, many of the faithful were martyred in the Yeonpung prison and execution ground."

"St. Luke Hwang Seok-du was born at Byeongbanggol in Yeonpung in 1813 and served as a catechist and translator of church books. In 1866 he was martyred at Galmaemot in Boryeong together with Bishop Daveluy, three missionaries and Jang Ju-gi."

"The remains of St. Hwang Seok-du were moved to Yeonpung Shrine in 1982, and the old Yeonpung county hall and a crucifix 8.5 meters high stand on the grounds. The shrine honors two histories in one place: the martyrdom site at the county office and the saint's hometown."
