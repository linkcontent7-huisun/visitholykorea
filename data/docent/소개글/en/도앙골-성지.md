"A village of believers grew at Doanggol through the preaching of Ludovico Yi Jon-chang, and in 1850 Father Choe Yang-eop recorded the poverty and persecution of its faithful in his first pastoral report. In 1866 villagers including John O were arrested and martyred at Gongju and Galmaemot."

"The deep valley of Geumji-ri, Naesan-myeon, Buyeo, was called Dowongok for its many wild peach trees, and the name became Doanggol. Linked over the mountain to the Sapti village by a secret path of the persecution years, the old trail joining these two shrines of lower Naepo is today's walking pilgrimage route."

"After the mission station closed in 1970, the Diocese of Daejeon blessed a house of prayer in 2011 to revive the pilgrimage space. Beside the monument raised that year praying for Father Choe Yang-eop's canonization stands an outdoor altar, and the open-air church honors the journey of the priest who wrote that first report."
