"Believers from Hongju and Yesan in Chungcheong, fleeing the Sinyu Persecution of 1801, founded a village on Meorusan. Discovered by constables in the Eulhae Persecution of 1815, Alexius Kim Si-u, Anna Yi Si-im, Simon Kim Gang-i and others were arrested or martyred elsewhere."

"The village stood in Posan-ri, Seokbo-myeon, Yeongyang, Gyeongsangbuk-do, deep in the mountains near the summit of Podosan in the Taebaek range. The name Meorusan came from a life of gathering wild grapes and kiwis and clearing fields, and the isolated terrain made a faith of refuge possible."

"After the persecution of 1815 the community scattered; the old station building did not survive and the traces of the village are faint. It is a pilgrimage site where one studies the mountain path and the site itself near the Posan-ri village hall, remembering the lives of Kim Si-u, Yi Si-im and Kim Gang-i, beatified in 2014."
