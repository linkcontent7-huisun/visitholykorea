"Hwangsaebawi in Gongju was the execution ground where believers of Chungcheong were put to death from the Sinhae Persecution of 1791 into the 1890s. The martyrs confirmed by name in records number 248, and St. Thomas Son Ja-seon was also martyred here."

"The martyrs' tower at the top of the shrine is shaped like two hands joined in prayer, and the names of the martyrs are engraved in the stone-tomb chapel. Twelve stones of light illuminate the pilgrimage path so that even those who died nameless are remembered."

"Two explanations of the name Hwangsaebawi survive: that storks often perched on the rock, and that it comes from the hangswae, the cangue fastened around prisoners' necks. This hill near the Gongju gamyeong was transformed from a place of public execution into a shrine honoring the martyrs."
