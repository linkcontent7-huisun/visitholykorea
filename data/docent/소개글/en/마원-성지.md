"Matthias Bak Sang-geun, a clerk of Mungyeong, met Father Calais at the Hansil village in 1866 and helped him escape. Though he vowed not to leave the priest's side, he finally returned home, was arrested, and was martyred by hanging in the Sangju prison; he was beatified in 2014."

"Mawon-ri is a mountain village near the gate of Mungyeong Saejae, where believers from Chungcheong crossed over after the Sinyu Persecution of 1801 and formed the Mawon village by clearing fields. When the Byeongin Persecution crossed the pass, the villagers were taken to Chungju, Sangju and Daegu for interrogation."

"Above the parking lot of Mawon Shrine lies the grave of Matthias Bak Sang-geun, and a statue expresses the friendship of Father Calais and Bak Sang-geun. Beside the statue unveiled in 1996 stand a statue of the risen Christ, a statue of Mary and a Way of the Cross, showing the last meeting of the two men."
