"St. Joseph's Seminary, begun at Baeron in 1855, closed in the Byeongin Persecution. It reopened in 1885 as the Sacred Heart Seminary at Bueonggol in Yeoju, and when Bishop Blanc secured the Yongsan site and moved it here in 1887, the formation of priests continued."

"The seminary site stands on a hill on Wonhyo-ro in Yongsan, overlooking Saenamteo and Danggogae. That a house of priestly formation was built close to the two places where clergy and believers were martyred during the persecutions shows that the Korean Church set out again upon the memory of martyrdom."

"The seminary building was completed first in 1891, followed by the Sacred Heart Church in 1902. Red brick and pointed arches form the Western exterior of both buildings, and the old seminary and church, now preserved within Sacred Heart Girls' High School, are each designated national historic sites."
