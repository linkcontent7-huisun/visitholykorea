"The Yangju county office is where believers lost their lives for their faith during the Byeongin Persecution. The Chimyeong Ilgi records that in 1866 the couple John Kim Yun-o and Martha Kwon, Mary Kim and Mr. Bak were martyred. Augustine Hong Seong-won was martyred in 1868, and the shrine remembers these five together."

"The shrine in Yuyang-dong lies on the road leading to the Yangju Confucian school and the Yangju Byeolsandae mask-dance ground. It was created by seeking the traces of martyrdom in the town center where the Joseon-era Yangju office stood. Following testimony that a martyrdom marker had once stood there, the Diocese of Uijeongbu confirmed the site and proclaimed it a shrine in 2016."

"In front of the Church of the Way to Heaven stands a sculpture shaped like the wooden cangue worn by the martyrs. The dark red exterior walls represent the martyrs' blood, and seen from the sky the whole building forms a cross. Inside, a sacred painting of the Yangju martyrs' shrine by the artist Sim Sun-hwa continues the story of martyrdom held in the building."
