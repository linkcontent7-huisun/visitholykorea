"At the Left and Right Police Bureaus around Jongno, believers including Francis Choe Gyeong-hwan and Peter Yu Dae-cheol were martyred after torture and imprisonment. Entrusted with the memory of persecution at these two bureaus, the Uigeumbu and the Jeonokseo, Jongno Church was designated the police-bureau pilgrimage church in 2013."

"The church is in Inui-dong, Jongno-gu, Seoul, close to the site of the former Dansungsa theater where the Left Police Bureau once stood. As the base of the police-bureau pilgrimage route, which walks from Myeongdong Cathedral past the office sites of Jongno to Yakhyeon Church, it links the martyrdom sites of the city center together."

"In the church basement is the Police Bureau Martyrs' Memorial Hall, opened in 2013, which displays instruments of torture and records of martyrdom. The bronze relief Passion and Glory on the outer wall and the ceiling reliefs of St. John Mary Vianney and the twelve apostles inside are also architectural elements honoring the police-bureau martyrs."
