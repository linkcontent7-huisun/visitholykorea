"At Jinmuyeong in 1868, Jang Chi-seon and Choe Yeong-jun were martyred on the charge of colluding with Westerners. The two had helped Father Ridel escape to China during the Byeongin Persecution. Their action to save the remaining believers was taken by a court grown wary after the French expedition of 1866 as collusion with foreign powers."

"Jinmuyeong was a garrison established under King Sukjong to defend Ganghwa Island and its coasts. The martyrs' shrine is now within Ganghwa Church in Gwancheong-ri, Ganghwa-eup, and the exact boundaries of the old garrison have not been fixed. The Good News shrine guide estimates the Jinmuyeong site as the area stretching from the former Ganghwa agricultural cooperative to Ganghwa Church."

"Ganghwa parish created the Jinmuyeong martyrs' shrine on the church grounds in 2004. An outdoor altar and a cross were set up as a space remembering the martyrdom of Jang Chi-seon and Choe Yeong-jun. It is a memorial that allows the history of persecution at the garrison guarding the Ganghwa coast to be recalled today in the churchyard of Ganghwa."
