"Believers who had hidden on Deokgasan came down to Daean-ri when freedom of religion was granted in 1886 and formed a village of faith. The mission station is said to have begun around 1892, and it is counted the oldest community in the Diocese of Wonju after Pungsuwon parish."

"The station stands on a small hill in Daean-ri, Heungeop-myeon, Wonju, Gangwon-do. Set between the farmland southwest of downtown Wonju and Deokgasan, its terrain shows the believers' move from mountain refuge to village community."

"The timber-framed hanok station was built in the early 1900s and blessed by Bishop Mutel in 1910, and became Registered Cultural Heritage No. 140 in 2004. The nave, sanctuary and sacristy sit under one roof, and a partition for confession remains in front of the sacristy."
