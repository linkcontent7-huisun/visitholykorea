"Supjeongi in Jeonju, named the head of the woods for its thick forest, was where soldiers trained in martial arts. From the Sinyu Persecution of 1801 through the Gihae Persecution of 1839 and the Byeongin Persecution of 1866, it was used as the execution ground for believers."

"The virgin couple John Yu Jung-cheol and Lutgarda Yi Sun-i were martyred here in 1801. Seven, including Yi Sun-i and Yu Jung-seong, were beatified in 2014, and six, including Jeong Mun-ho and Son Seon-ji, were canonized in 1984."

"The Church bought and preserved the site in the 1930s, raising a memorial cross in 1935 and a memorial tower in 1968. At the present shrine, moved in 1992 because of urban development, an outdoor altar, a statue of Mary and a Way of the Cross were added in 2004."
