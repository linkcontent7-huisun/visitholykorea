"Darakgol is the village of believers where St. Francis Choe Gyeong-hwan and his son Father Thomas Choe Yang-eop were born. Choe Gyeong-hwan died in prison in 1839 and his wife Mary Yi Seong-rye was martyred the following year, while Choe Yang-eop, who walked the whole country, is called the martyr of sweat."

"On a ridge in Nongam-ri, Hwaseong-myeon, Cheongyang, the graves of unknown martyrs lie in several rows. Oral tradition, passed down with these row graves, tells that the bodies of believers martyred at the Hongju provincial office in 1866 were secretly carried out by night and buried on the Choe family's hill."

"A memorial chapel was built in front of the nameless martyrs' row graves in 2008. The Father Choe Yang-eop memorial hall, opened in 2019, exhibits the lives of the father and son born at Darakgol and the pastoral journeys of Choe Yang-eop, linking the pilgrimage route between the church and the row graves."
