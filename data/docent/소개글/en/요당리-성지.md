"The Yodang-ri village of believers was formed by those who fled from Seoul and Naepo after the Sinyu Persecution of 1801. St. Joseph Jang Ju-gi and Blessed Thomas Jang were born here, and the footsteps of Bishop Imbert and Stephen Min Geuk-ga also remain."

"Yodang-ri in Yanggam-myeon, Hwaseong, was a village where seawater came in at high tide and ponds of water pepper were common until the late Joseon era. Thanks to its open waterways it became a mission bridgehead linking Naepo in Chungcheong, inland Gyeonggi and Seoul, and the Yanggan station was connected to the Gatdeungi and Eunyi stations."

"From 2006 the Diocese of Suwon laid out a chapel and a rosary path, and in 2010 consecrated the red-brick cathedral. The vermilion bricks of the sanctuary, the tabernacle of the martyr saints, and the stained glass turning from red to blue express martyrdom and the water of life."
