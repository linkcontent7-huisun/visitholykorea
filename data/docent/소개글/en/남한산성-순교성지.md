"Namhansanseong was a military fortress of Joseon and an administrative center where the governor of Gwangju held trials. During the Byeongin Persecution, Catholics arrested across southern Gyeonggi were sent to the fortress prison, where they died in custody or were executed."

"Believers were dragged into the fortress through the east gate, and some were sent on to Saenamteo in Seoul after trial. The traces of the prison and execution ground inside the walls show the military history of the fortress together with the judicial process of the persecution years."

"Namhansanseong is a fortress heritage inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 2014. Along the martyrs' route, walking past the gates and pavilions, you can retrace the imprisonment and martyrdom of believers that took place inside a national defense installation."
