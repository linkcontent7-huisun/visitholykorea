"Gaegap Market is where Matthias Choe Yeo-gyeom of Mujang was martyred in the Sinyu Persecution of 1801. Arrested while spreading the faith among his neighbors, he was interrogated at Mujang, Jeonju and Seoul. Under the sentence that he be sent home for execution, he was beheaded at this market, and in 2014 he was declared blessed."

"Gaegap Market in Seokgyo-ri, Gongeum-myeon, Gochang, stood on the road linking the town of Mujang with Beopseongpo in Yeonggwang. The market, where goods from many districts gathered, was also used for supplies and communication by the righteous armies of the late Joseon era. It was later closed under Japanese rule and the market vanished, but its name survived as the place of Choe Yeo-gyeom's martyrdom."

"The shrine holds a twelve-meter martyrs' memorial tower and an outdoor altar. After the blessing ceremony of 2013, work continued, and in 2015 a marker at Blessed Choe Yeo-gyeom's martyrdom site and a Resurrection Garden were added. The Way of the Cross and the tower have created a new pilgrimage route through the old market."
