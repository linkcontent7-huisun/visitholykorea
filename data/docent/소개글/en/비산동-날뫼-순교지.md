"The Nalmoe martyrdom site in Bisan-dong is said to be the site of a Joseon-era execution ground in Daegu, where Catholics are believed to have lost their lives during the persecutions. However, the names and number of those martyred here, and the exact dates, have not been confirmed in written records."

"Nalmoe is the old name of Bisan-dong in Daegu's Seo-gu district, where the site lies. The Nalmoe area held the village of believers that Father Choe Yang-eop mentioned in his letters, and the family of Yi Eung-chil buried and guarded the body of St. John Yi Yun-il, martyred at Gwandeokjeong, on a nearby hill."

"No old execution structures survive, and this is a martyrdom site that remembers the execution ground itself rather than a church building. Since no separate memorial building or list of martyrs has been confirmed, the site marker and the records of the Archdiocese of Daegu await further verification."
