"Songol, on the slopes of Gwanggyosan in Yongin, was a village of about a dozen households of believers and a place where missionaries of the Paris Foreign Missions prepared for their ministry. Fathers Daveluy and Aumaître, among others, learned the Korean language and customs here."

"St. Henri Dorie was arrested at Songol on 27 February 1866 and martyred at Saenamteo in Seoul on 7 March. The shrine holds a statue of Father Dorie, a martyrs' monument, a chapel and a memorial hall."

"For the centenary of the martyrdom in 1966, a cross was raised, made from a millstone once used by Father Dorie's father in his home village in France. In 2013 the remains of four unknown martyrs of Songol were moved here and a martyrs' path was laid out."
